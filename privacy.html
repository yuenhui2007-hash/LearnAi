1. Overview

LearnAI is an AI-powered educational platform providing free and premium study resources for Cambridge IGCSE, A-Level, IB, and IELTS students. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using LearnAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in via Google or Apple, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Authentication provider (local, Google, or Apple)
  • Unique provider ID (for OAuth users)

Test & Progress Data

When you take practice tests, we store:

  • Test scores and completion times
  • Answers submitted (for review and feedback)
  • Skill breakdowns and performance analytics
  • Study plan preferences

Usage Data

We collect anonymised usage statistics to improve the platform, including:

  • Pages visited and features used
  • Device type and browser information
  • General geographic region (derived from IP, not stored)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To identify you and keep your account secure.
  • Progress Tracking: To display your test history, scores, and personalised study plans.
  • AI Feedback: To generate subject-specific marking and improvement suggestions.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyse usage patterns and optimise content and features.
  • Communication: To send essential service notifications (never marketing without consent).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Google & Apple Sign-In

We offer sign-in through Google and Apple as a convenience. When you use these services:

  • Google: We receive your email, name, and Google ID via Google's Identity Services. Google may collect additional data per their own privacy policy. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: We receive your email and Apple ID via Apple's Sign In with Apple service. Apple may collect additional data per their own privacy policy. See Apple Privacy Policy.

We never receive or store your Google or Apple password. All OAuth tokens are verified server-side and handled securely.

5. Local Browser Storage

Some features store data directly in your browser using localStorage:

  • Authentication token (encrypted JWT)
  • Cached user profile
  • Test progress and draft answers (before submission)
  • Theme preferences

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly submit a test or log in. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

6. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Authentication cookie (token): HTTP-only, secure, SameSite. Used to maintain your login session. Expires after 7 days.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text).
  • Authentication tokens are signed JWTs with server-side verification.
  • Rate limiting prevents brute-force attacks on login and OAuth endpoints.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers block XSS and injection attacks.
  • Strict Transport Security (HSTS) enforces HTTPS connections.
  • Cross-origin requests are restricted to authorised frontend domains only.

9. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Test scores: Retained for progress tracking. Deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Usage logs: Anonymised after 90 days. Raw logs deleted after 12 months.
  • localStorage: Retained on your device until you clear your browser data.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Export your test scores and progress data.
  • Objection: Opt out of non-essential data processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

LearnAI is designed for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. Significant changes will be announced via a notice on the website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
