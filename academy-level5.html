Level 5 of 6

Department-Specific AI

Personalised AI training for HR, Finance, Marketing, Sales, Support, Operations, Legal, IT, and Executive roles. Estimated time: 4 hours.

Module 1: Human Resources

👥 HR AI Applications

  • Recruitment: Screen CVs, draft job descriptions, create interview questions
  • Onboarding: Generate checklists, welcome emails, training schedules
  • Performance Reviews: Draft feedback, set SMART goals, track progress
  • Policies: Write and update HR policies, explain them to employees
  • Conflict Resolution: Draft mediation scripts, investigate procedures

🎯 Practice Task

Upload a job description. Ask AI to: (1) improve it, (2) create 5 interview questions, (3) draft an offer letter.

Module 2: Finance

💰 Finance AI Applications

  • Reports: Generate executive summaries from financial data
  • Excel Analysis: Write formulas, find anomalies, create pivot tables
  • Budgeting: Variance analysis, forecasting, scenario planning
  • Audit: Check transactions for irregularities, compliance checks
  • Presentations: Create board-ready financial decks

🎯 Practice Task

Upload a CSV of 50 transactions. Ask AI to identify anomalies and categorise spending.

Module 3: Marketing & Sales

📢 Marketing AI Applications

  • Campaigns: Generate ideas, write copy, plan calendars
  • SEO: Keyword research, meta descriptions, content optimisation
  • Social Media: Create posts, schedule content, analyse trends
  • Email Marketing: Subject lines, A/B tests, drip campaigns
  • Customer Personas: Build detailed buyer profiles

🤝 Sales AI Applications

  • Prospecting: Research leads, draft cold emails, LinkedIn messages
  • Proposals: Generate customised proposals, pricing tables
  • Objection Handling: Prepare responses to common objections
  • CRM Updates: Summarise calls, update records, follow-up reminders

Module 4: Customer Service

🎧 Support AI Applications

  • Ticket Responses: Draft empathetic, accurate replies
  • Escalation: Identify when to escalate, draft handover notes
  • Knowledge Base: Create and update help articles
  • Sentiment Analysis: Detect angry customers early
  • Quality Assurance: Review conversations for compliance

🎯 Roleplay Challenge

Use Claw to practice handling: (1) angry customer demanding refund, (2) confused user needing technical help, (3) customer asking for exception to policy.

Module 5: Operations, Legal & IT

⚙️ Operations AI Applications

  • SOPs: Write, update, and optimise standard operating procedures
  • Workflows: Map processes, identify bottlenecks, suggest improvements
  • Project Planning: Gantt charts, risk registers, resource allocation
  • Meeting Minutes: Auto-summarise, extract action items

⚖️ Legal AI Applications (Assistive Only)

  • Document Review: Summarise contracts, highlight key clauses
  • Compliance: Check against regulations, flag risks
  • Research: Find relevant case law, legislation
  • Disclaimer: AI assists but does NOT replace qualified legal review

💻 IT AI Applications

  • Documentation: Write technical docs, API references
  • Debugging: Analyse error logs, suggest fixes
  • Scripts: Generate automation scripts, SQL queries
  • Knowledge Management: Organise wiki, FAQ, troubleshooting guides

Module 6: Executive Leadership

👔 Executive AI Applications

  • Strategy: SWOT analysis, competitive analysis, market research
  • Board Reports: Executive summaries, KPI dashboards
  • Speeches: Keynote drafts, town hall presentations
  • Decision Support: Scenario modelling, risk assessment
  • Stakeholder Comms: Investor updates, partnership proposals

📋 Final Project

Choose your department. Complete a real work task using AI: write a document, analyse data, or prepare a presentation. Submit your AI-assisted output.
