IELTS Speaking

3parts ° 11-14 minutes ³ Face-to-face with examiner

Part 1: Introduction & Interview (4-5 minutes)

The examiner introduces themselves and confirms your identity.

They ask general questions on familiar topics such as:

  • Home, family, work, studies, interests
  • Daily routines, hobbies, hometown
  • Preferences and opinions on common topics

Part 2: Individual Long Turn (3-4 minutes)

You receive a task card with a topic and prompts. You have 1 minute to prepare notes, then speak for 1-2 minutes.

Example topic: "Describe a memorable journey you have taken."

The examiner may ask one or two follow-up questions.

Part 3: Two-Way Discussion (4-5 minutes)

The examiner asks further questions connected to the Part 2 topic.

These questions are more abstract and require you to express and justify opinions.

Example: "How has travel changed in your country over the last 50 years?"

Assessment Criteria

  • Fluency & Coherence: Ability to speak at length without hesitation
  • Lexical Resource: Vocabulary range and accuracy
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: Sentence structure variety
  • Pronunciation: Clear speech with natural intonation

⏇�� Top Tips

  • Speak naturally — DON'T MEMORIND long scripts
  • Use the 1-minute preparation time wisely ´ jot down key points
  • Extend your answers with reasons and examples
  • Do It okay to ask the examiner to repeat a question
