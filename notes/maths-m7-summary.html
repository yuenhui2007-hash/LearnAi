Differentiation

9709 · Last-Minute Revision

📝 Quick Revision Summary

  • d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹
  • Product rule: d(uv)/dx = u dv/dx + v du/dx
  • Quotient rule: d(u/v)/dx = (v du/dx − u dv/dx)/v²
  • Chain rule: dy/dx = dy/du × du/dx
  • Stationary points: f'(x) = 0; max if f'' < 0, min if f'' > 0
  • Always show working clearly — method marks are awarded for correct steps even if the final answer is wrong.
  • Check your answers by substitution where possible.
  • Manage your time: don't spend too long on any single question.

💡 Quick Exam Tips

  • Always simplify before differentiating if possible.
  • When using the product or quotient rule, identify u and v clearly.
  • For stationary points, always find the y-coordinate as well as the x-coordinate.
  • Use the second derivative test when possible — it is faster than testing intervals.
  • In connected rates problems, identify which rate is given and which is required.
  • Read each question carefully — underline key words and constraints.
  • Sketch diagrams even when not explicitly asked; they help organise your thinking.
  • Leave time at the end to review answers and catch arithmetic errors.
