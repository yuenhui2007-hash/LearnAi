Impact of ICT on Society

Topic i9 — Revision Summary

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • ICT impacts: Communication, education, healthcare, employment, entertainment, government — all transformed, with benefits and risks.
  • Digital divide: Access, use, and quality gaps. Solutions: infrastructure, education, subsidies.
  • Health: RSI, eye strain, mental health issues. Mitigate with ergonomics, breaks, limits.
  • Environment: E-waste and data centre energy are major concerns. Positive: dematerialization, smart grids.
  • Privacy: GDPR principles, individual rights, lawful processing, consent requirements.
  • Laws: Computer Misuse Act (hacking, malware), Copyright Act (software piracy).
  • Ethics: Surveillance, censorship, AI bias, automation unemployment, digital footprints.
  • Globalisation: Offshoring, e-commerce, cryptocurrencies. Benefits and challenges.
  • Misinformation: Fake news, deepfakes, echo chambers. Counter with literacy and fact-checking.
  • Evaluation framework: Technical, Economic, Social, Ethical, Legal, Environmental, Security (TESELES).
  • Exam tip: Always present BALANCED arguments — benefits AND drawbacks with real-world examples.

🔑 Key Terms

  • Digital divide, E-government, Telemedicine, Gig economy, E-waste, Dematerialization
  • GDPR, Consent, Right to erasure, Data portability, Data controller, Data processor
  • Computer Misuse Act, Copyright, Patent, Trademark, Trade secret, Creative Commons
  • Surveillance, Censorship, Filter bubble, Echo chamber, Deepfake, Fake news
  • Offshoring, Outsourcing, E-commerce, Cryptocurrency, Blockchain, UBI
  • Industry 4.0, Automation, Reskilling, Media literacy, Fact-checking

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Use SPECIFIC real-world examples (M-Pesa, GDPR fines, deepfake incidents) to strengthen essays.
  • For evaluation questions, use a structured framework — list criteria, assess each, conclude.
  • Balance is key: never write only positives or only negatives.
  • Link legal issues to named legislation; don't just say "it's illegal."
