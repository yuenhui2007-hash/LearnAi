Hitler's Germany, 1929–1947

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1929 Crash → depression → Nazis rise (107 seats 1930; 230 July 1932).
  • 30 Jan 1933: Hitler Chancellor. Reichstag Fire (27 Feb) → Emergency Decree → Enabling Act (23 March).
  • 1934: Night of Long Knives (30 June) — SA purged; Hindenburg dies (2 Aug) → Hitler Führer.
  • 1933–39: Gleichschaltung; propaganda; Hitler Youth (compulsory 1936); Nuremberg Laws (1935); recovery via rearmament/Autobahns.
  • Foreign policy: Rhineland (1936); Anschluss (1938); Munich (Sept 1938); rest of Czechoslovakia (March 1939); Nazi-Soviet Pact (Aug 1939); Poland invaded (1 Sept 1939).
  • War: Blitzkrieg; Battle of Britain; Barbarossa (June 1941); Stalingrad (Feb 1943); D-Day (June 1944); Hitler suicide (30 April 1945).
  • Nuremberg Trials (1945–46): 12 sentenced to death; Hess life imprisonment.
  • Historiography: Totalitarian vs polycratic (Broszat/Kershaw); Intentionalist vs Structuralist foreign policy (Hildebrand vs Mommsen/Mason).
  • Exam Tip: Know exact sequence 1933→1934→1935→1936→1938→1939. Reference Hitler's appointment as elite miscalculation, not inevitable.
