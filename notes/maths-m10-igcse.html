Basic Probability

P(A) = n(A)/n(ℰ)
P(A') = 1 − P(A)
P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
Worked Example

P(A) = 0.4, P(B) = 0.5, P(A ∩ B) = 0.2. Find P(A ∪ B).

P(A ∪ B) = 0.4 + 0.5 − 0.2 = 0.7

2

Conditional Probability

P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B)/P(B)
Independent: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
3

Permutations and Combinations

ⁿPᵣ = n!/(n−r)! (order matters)
ⁿCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!] (order doesn't matter)
Worked Example

How many ways to choose 3 people from 10?

¹⁰C₃ = 10!/(3!×7!) = 120

📝 Quick Summary

  • P(A') = 1 − P(A)
  • P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
  • P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B|A)
  • Independent: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
  • ⁿCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!]

💡 Exam Tips

  • Draw tree diagrams for multi-stage problems
  • Distinguish permutation (order matters) vs combination
  • Venn diagrams help visualise unions and intersections
  • For "at least one", use complement: 1 − P(none)
