Geometry & Trigonometry

IB DP Mathematics: Applications & Interpretation HL/SL — Topic 3

Overview

Geometry and Trigonometry (Topic 3) in Maths AI covers practical measurement, Voronoi diagrams, and trigonometric applications. The focus is on real-world problem solving rather than abstract proof.

3.1 Volume & Surface Area

Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, A = 4πr²
Cylinder: V = πr²h, A = 2πr² + 2πrh
Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h
Pyramid: V = (1/3) × base area × height

3.2 Voronoi Diagrams

  • Partition a plane into regions based on distance to a set of seed points.
  • Each region contains all points closer to its seed than to any other.
  • Edges are perpendicular bisectors of lines joining seed points.
  • Vertices are equidistant from three (or more) seeds.

Applications: nearest service location, territorial analysis, ecology.

3.3 Trigonometry

Sine rule: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C
Cosine rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A
Area = ½ab sin C

Bearings: measured clockwise from north.
Angle of elevation: angle above horizontal.
Angle of depression: angle below horizontal.

Common Misconceptions

  • Voronoi diagrams: The edge between two seeds is the perpendicular bisector, not just any line.
  • Bearings: Always measured clockwise from north and given as three digits (e.g., 045°).
  • Sine rule ambiguity: When given two sides and a non-included angle, there may be two possible triangles.

Exam-Specific Information

  • Paper 1: 2–3 questions on volume, trigonometry, and Voronoi diagrams.
  • Paper 2: Contextual problems involving navigation, design, and measurement.
  • IA: Voronoi diagram applications (shop locations, wildlife territories) are popular.

Practice Questions

  • A spherical tank of radius 3 m is half full of water. Calculate the volume of water.
  • Three towns are located at coordinates A(0,0), B(4,0), and C(2,6). Construct the Voronoi diagram and find the vertex.
  • A ship sails 50 km on a bearing of 060°, then 80 km on a bearing of 150°. Find its distance and bearing from the starting point.

Last-Minute Summary

  • Know volume and surface area formulas for sphere, cylinder, cone, pyramid.
  • Voronoi: edges are perpendicular bisectors; vertices equidistant from 3+ seeds.
  • Sine rule: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C.
  • Cosine rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A.
  • Bearings: clockwise from north; three digits.

Exam Tips

  • Read each question carefully and identify the command term before answering.
  • Show all working — method marks are generous in IB Mathematics.
  • Give answers to 3 significant figures unless specified otherwise.
  • For Voronoi questions, draw perpendicular bisectors accurately.
  • For bearing questions, draw a clear diagram with north lines.
