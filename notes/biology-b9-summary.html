Gas Exchange & Respiration

Last-Minute Revision · 9700 (A-Level) · 0610 (IGCSE)

Last-Minute Revision

Key Points

  • Gas exchange: large SA, thin barrier, moist, good blood supply. Alveoli, gills (countercurrent), tracheae, stomata.
  • Breathing: inspiration (diaphragm contracts, intercostals contract, volume up, pressure down); expiration (reverse).
  • Glycolysis: cytoplasm; glucose → 2 pyruvate + 2 ATP + 2 NADH.
  • Link reaction: mitochondrial matrix; pyruvate → acetyl CoA + CO2 + NADH.
  • Krebs cycle: matrix; acetyl CoA → CO2 + NADH + FADH2 + ATP.
  • Oxidative phosphorylation: cristae; ETC pumps H+; chemiosmosis through ATP synthase; O2 final electron acceptor.
  • Anaerobic: mammals → lactate; yeast → ethanol + CO2. Purpose: regenerate NAD+ for glycolysis.
  • RQ values: carbohydrate = 1.0; lipid = 0.7; protein = 0.9.

Exam tip: Always state where each stage occurs: glycolysis = cytoplasm; link + Krebs = mitochondrial matrix; oxidative phosphorylation = inner membrane (cristae). In ETC questions, explain chemiosmosis: proton gradient → proton motive force → ATP synthase. For anaerobic, emphasise NAD+ regeneration is essential.
