IGCSE Content

1. SOH CAH TOA

\sin θ = \frac{opp}{hyp} \quad \cos θ = \frac{adj}{hyp} \quad \tan θ = \frac{opp}{adj}

2. Sine & Cosine Rules

\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} \quad a² = b² + c² - 2bc\cos A \quad Area = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C

3. Exact Values

  • sin 30° = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3
  • sin 45° = 1/√2, cos 45° = 1/√2, tan 45° = 1
  • sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3

4. Bearings & 3D Trigonometry

Bearings measured clockwise from North. For 3D problems, identify right-angled triangles within the shape.

AS Level Content

1. Radian Measure

π \text{ rad} = 180° \quad s = rθ \quad A = \frac{1}{2}r²θ

2. Trigonometric Identities

\tan θ = \frac{\sin θ}{\cos θ} \quad \sin² θ + \cos² θ = 1 \quad 1 + \tan² θ = \sec² θ

3. Solving Trigonometric Equations

\sin θ = 0.5 \Rightarrow θ = 30°, 150° \text{ (or } \frac{π}{6}, \frac{5π}{6}\text{ in radians)}

Use the CAST diagram to find all solutions in a given interval.

A2 Level Content

1. Compound & Double Angle Formulae

\sin(A±B) = \sin A\cos B ± \cos A\sin B \quad \cos(A±B) = \cos A\cos B ∓ \sin A\sin B
\sin 2A = 2\sin A\cos A \quad \cos 2A = \cos²A - \sin²A = 2\cos²A - 1 = 1 - 2\sin²A

2. R-Formula (a sin θ + b cos θ)

a\sin θ + b\cos θ = R\sin(θ + α) \quad R = \sqrt{a²+b²}, \quad \tan α = \frac{b}{a}

Last-Minute Summary

  • Area: ½ab sin C.
  • Radians: π = 180°. Arc = rθ, sector = ½r²θ.
  • Identities: tan = sin/cos, sin² + cos² = 1.
