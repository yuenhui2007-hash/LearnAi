Stalin's Russia, 1924–1953

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1924: Lenin dies; Stalin uses General Secretary position to build patronage.
  • 1924–29: Defeats Trotsky, Zinoviev, Kamenev, Bukharin. Trotsky exiled 1929.
  • 1928–32: First Five-Year Plan; collectivisation; dekulakisation. Famine 1932–33 (Holodomor): 5–7 million dead.
  • 1936–38: Great Terror — show trials (Zinoviev, Bukharin), army purges (Tukhachevsky), Yezhovshchina (700,000+ executed).
  • 1939: Nazi-Soviet Pact. 1941: Barbarossa.
  • 1942–43: Stalingrad turning point. 1945: Victory; ~27 million Soviet dead.
  • Post-war: Eastern European purges; Doctors' Plot (1952–53). Stalin dies 5 March 1953.
  • Historiography: Totalitarian (state controls all) vs Revisionist (social mobility, local initiative) vs Post-revisionist (Stalinism as civilisation — Kotkin).
  • Exam Tip: Sequence: power struggle → collectivisation/industrialisation → terror → war → post-war. Compare with Hitler where relevant. Know show trial names and years.
