Last-Minute Revision

🎯 Key Points to Remember

  • Protons = atomic number; neutrons = mass number - atomic number
  • Isotopes: same proton number, different neutron number
  • Electron configuration order: 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p...
  • Cr = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ and Cu = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (exceptions)
  • IE increases across period, decreases down group
  • Drops at Group 2→3 and Group 5→6 due to subshell change and pairing repulsion
  • Successive IE jumps indicate new shell being broken into
  • Mass spec: molecular ion = Mr; fragmentation helps identify structure
  • Transition metal ions lose 4s before 3d electrons

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Confusing atomic number and mass number
  • Writing 3d before 4s in electron configurations
  • Forgetting Hund's rule when filling p orbitals
  • Not explaining shielding AND distance when comparing IE
  • Forgetting that 4s fills before 3d but empties before 3d in ions
