Detailed Notes

1. Overview

Creative Writing covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IAL examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Narrative structure: exposition, rising action, climax, resolution
  • Character development: show not tell
  • Setting and atmosphere: descriptive techniques
  • Dialogue: formatting, purpose, subtext
  • Perspective and voice: first, second, third person
  • Literary devices: metaphor, simile, symbolism, foreshadowing
  • Editing and refining creative work

3. Important Formulas and Relationships

Key relationships and equations for Creative Writing are essential for problem-solving. Practice applying these to different contexts.

4. Common Exam Questions

Examiners frequently test:

  • Definitions and explanations of key terms
  • Application of concepts to novel situations
  • Analysis and evaluation of theories and models
  • Comparison between different approaches or periods

5. Practical Applications

Connect theoretical knowledge to real-world contexts. Use contemporary examples and case studies where appropriate to demonstrate deeper understanding.

  • Read questions carefully - identify command words (describe, explain, evaluate, compare)
  • Use diagrams and labelled illustrations where appropriate
  • Structure extended answers with clear paragraphs and topic sentences
  • Include specific examples and data to support arguments
  • Check your answers for accuracy in calculations and spelling of technical terms
  • Manage your time: allocate time proportionally to marks available
