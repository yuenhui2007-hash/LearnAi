IGCSE AS A2

The French Revolution, 1774–1794

history-h7

📘 IGCSE Content

Causes

  • Social: Three Estates system — clergy (1%), nobility (2%), commoners (97%)
  • Economic: Debt from wars (American Revolution, Seven Years' War); bad harvests 1787–88
  • Political: Absolute monarchy; Estates-General not called since 1614
  • Intellectual: Enlightenment ideas — Rousseau, Voltaire, Montesquieu

Key Events

  • 5 May 1789: Estates-General convenes at Versailles
  • 20 June 1789: Tennis Court Oath — Third Estate forms National Assembly
  • 14 July 1789: Storming of the Bastille
  • 26 August 1789: Declaration of Rights of Man and Citizen
  • 1792: Monarchy abolished; Louis XVI executed (21 Jan 1793)
  • 1793–94: Reign of Terror — Robespierre, Committee of Public Safety, guillotine

📗 AS Level Content

Revolutionary Government

  • Constitutional Monarchy (1789–92): Limited royal power; National Assembly
  • Girondins vs Jacobins: Moderate republicans vs radical revolutionaries
  • sans-culottes: Urban working class; demanded price controls and direct democracy

Reforms

  • Abolition of feudalism (4 Aug 1789)
  • Civil Constitution of the Clergy (1790) — state control of Church
  • Metric system introduced

📙 A2 Level Content

Terror & Thermidor

  • Law of Suspects (1793): Anyone suspected of opposing the revolution could be arrested
  • Robespierre's fall (July 1794): Thermidorian Reaction; executed 9 Thermidor
  • Directory (1795–99): Five-man executive; corruption and instability

Historiography

  • Marxist: Bourgeois revolution against feudalism
  • Cobban: Not a bourgeois revolution — social groups not clearly defined
  • Furet: Ideological revolution; importance of political culture

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Causes: Social inequality, debt, Enlightenment ideas
  • 1789: Estates-General → Tennis Court Oath → Bastille (14 July)
  • 1789: Declaration of Rights of Man
  • 1792: Republic declared; Louis XVI executed
  • 1793–94: Reign of Terror; Robespierre
  • 1794: Thermidorian Reaction; Directory established
