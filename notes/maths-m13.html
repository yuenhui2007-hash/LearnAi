Kinematics in One Dimension

Kinematics describes motion without considering forces. The variables are displacement (s), velocity (v), acceleration (a), and time (t).

v = u + at
s = ut + 12at²
s = (u + v)2t
v² = u² + 2as

where u = initial velocity, v = final velocity

Worked Example

A car accelerates from rest at 3 m/s² for 5 seconds. Find final velocity and distance.

v = 0 + 3(5) = 15 m/s

s = 0 + 12(3)(25) = 37.5 m

Worked Example

A ball is thrown upwards at 20 m/s. Find max height (g = 10 m/s²).

At max height, v = 0. v² = u² + 2as → 0 = 400 − 20s → s = 20 m

Key Point: Choose a positive direction and stick to it. If an object moves opposite to your chosen positive direction, its velocity and displacement will be negative.

Calculus in Kinematics

v = dsdt
a = dvdt = d²sdt²

Conversely:

s = ∫ v dt
v = ∫ a dt
Worked Example

A particle has velocity v = 3t² − 4t + 2. Find displacement after 3 seconds, given s = 0 when t = 0.

s = ∫(3t² − 4t + 2)dt = t³ − 2t² + 2t + c

s(0) = 0 → c = 0

s(3) = 27 − 18 + 6 = 15 m

Worked Example — Finding Distance

For v = t² − 4t + 3, find distance travelled from t = 0 to t = 3.

v = (t−1)(t−3). v = 0 at t = 1 and t = 3.

From 0 to 1: v > 0. s₁ = [t³/3 − 2t² + 3t]₀¹ = 1/3 − 2 + 3 = 4/3

From 1 to 3: v < 0. s₂ = |[t³/3 − 2t² + 3t]₁³| = |(9−18+9) − (1/3−2+3)| = 4/3

Total distance = 8/3 m

Key Point: Distance is the total path length, so integrate |v|. Displacement is the net change in position, so integrate v directly. When v changes sign, split the integral.

Kinematics in Two Dimensions

Motion in a plane can be described using vector components.

Horizontal: sₓ = ut cos θ, vₓ = u cos θ (constant)
Vertical: sᵧ = ut sin θ − 12gt², vᵧ = u sin θ − gt
Worked Example — Projectile

A ball is projected at 25 m/s at 30° above horizontal. Find max height and range.

uₓ = 25 cos 30° = 21.65 m/s, uᵧ = 25 sin 30° = 12.5 m/s

Max height: vᵧ = 0 → 0 = 12.5 − 10t → t = 1.25 s

sᵧ = 12.5(1.25) − 5(1.25)² = 7.81 m

Range: time of flight = 2 × 1.25 = 2.5 s

Range = 21.65 × 2.5 = 54.1 m

Exam-Style Question

Question: A particle is projected from ground level and just clears a 10 m wall 20 m away. Find the initial speed and angle of projection. [8]

Solution: Using trajectory equation: y = x tan θ − gx²2u² cos² θ. Substituting (20, 10) and solving simultaneously with the condition that the particle reaches max height at the wall (symmetric trajectory): u ≈ 19.8 m/s at 45° (M1, M1, A1, A1, M1, M1, A1, A1)

📝 Quick Summary

  • v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as
  • v = ds/dt; a = dv/dt
  • Projectile: horizontal velocity constant
  • Max height when vertical velocity = 0
  • Range = horizontal velocity × time of flight

💡 Exam Tips

  • Always choose a positive direction and be consistent with signs.
  • For projectile motion, horizontal velocity is constant (no air resistance).
  • Split projectile motion into horizontal and vertical components.
  • Time of flight depends only on vertical motion.
  • When using calculus, remember distance = ∫|v| and displacement = ∫v.
