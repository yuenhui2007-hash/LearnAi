Vectors

9709 · Last-Minute Revision

📝 Quick Revision Summary

  • Magnitude |a| = √(a₁² + a₂² + a₃²)
  • Dot product: a · b = |a||b| cos θ = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃
  • Perpendicular vectors: a · b = 0
  • Line: r = a + λb
  • Cartesian: (x−a₁)/b₁ = (y−a₂)/b₂ = (z−a₃)/b₃
  • Always show working clearly — method marks are awarded for correct steps even if the final answer is wrong.
  • Check your answers by substitution where possible.
  • Manage your time: don't spend too long on any single question.

💡 Quick Exam Tips

  • Always draw a diagram for vector problems.
  • The dot product a · b = 0 means the vectors are perpendicular.
  • For line equations, the direction vector can be scaled by any non-zero constant.
  • When finding intersections, equate components and solve simultaneously.
  • Remember: vectors have magnitude and direction, but position vectors also have a specific location.
  • Read each question carefully — underline key words and constraints.
  • Sketch diagrams even when not explicitly asked; they help organise your thinking.
  • Leave time at the end to review answers and catch arithmetic errors.
