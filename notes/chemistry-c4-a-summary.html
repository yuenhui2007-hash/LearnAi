C4: Electrochemistry

9701 (A-Level) · Last-Minute Revision

📚 Comprehensive Revision Summary

Key Formulae:
• See main notes for detailed equations and calculations
  • Electrolysis: decomposition by DC; cations → cathode (reduction), anions → anode (oxidation)
  • Molten electrolysis: one cation/anion → products are elements (e.g., NaCl → Na + Cl₂)
  • Aqueous electrolysis: consider water competition; less reactive metals deposit, H₂ from water for reactive metals
  • Standard electrode potential: measured against SHE (E° = 0.00 V); more positive = stronger oxidising agent
  • E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode; positive = spontaneous
  • Quantitative: Q = It; n(e⁻) = Q/F; F = 96500 C mol⁻¹
  • Fuel cells: H₂ + O₂ → H₂O; efficient but H₂ storage is challenging

💡 Exam Tips

  • In electrolysis, cathode = reduction (negative in electrolysis, positive in galvanic cells)
  • Balance electrons when combining half-equations; state symbols matter
  • Time must be in seconds for Q = It
  • E°cell predicts feasibility, NOT rate — kinetics may prevent reaction
