Cognitive Psychology

Detailed Notes

1. Memory Models

  • Multi-store: Sensory → Short-term → Long-term. Rehearsal transfers STM to LTM.
  • Working memory: Central executive, phonological loop, visuospatial sketchpad, episodic buffer.

2. Forgetting

  • Decay: Trace fades over time.
  • Displacement: New information pushes out old (STM).
  • Interference: Proactive (old disrupts new) or retroactive (new disrupts old).
  • Retrieval failure: Missing cues.

3. Eyewitness Testimony

Leading questions, post-event information, anxiety, age all affect accuracy.

4. Thinking and Decision-Making

Heuristics (mental shortcuts): availability, representativeness, anchoring. Cognitive biases.

Last-Minute Summary

  • Multi-store: sensory → STM → LTM
  • Working memory: central executive + phonological loop + visuospatial sketchpad
  • Forgetting: decay, displacement, interference, retrieval failure
  • Heuristics: availability, representativeness, anchoring
