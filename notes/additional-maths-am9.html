Arc Length and Sector Area

Arc length: s = rθ
Sector area: A = 12r²θ
Segment area = 12r²(θ − sin θ)
Worked Example

A sector has radius 10 cm and angle 0.8 rad. Find arc length and area.

s = 10 × 0.8 = 8 cm

A = 12 × 100 × 0.8 = 40 cm²

Worked Example — Segment

Find the area of the minor segment for a chord subtending π/3 at the centre (r = 6).

Segment = 12 × 36 × (π3 − sin π3) = 18(π3 − √32) = 6π − 9√3

Key Point: All arc and sector formulas require θ in radians. Convert degrees to radians first if necessary.

📝 Quick Summary

  • π rad = 180°
  • Arc length s = rθ
  • Sector area A = ½r²θ
  • Segment area = ½r²(θ − sin θ)
  • θ must be in radians

💡 Exam Tips

  • Always convert to radians before using arc/sector formulas.
  • Learn the common degree-radian conversions.
  • Segment area = sector area − triangle area.
  • For perimeter of sector: P = 2r + rθ.
