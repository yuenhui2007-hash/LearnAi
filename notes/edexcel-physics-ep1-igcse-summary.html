Detailed Notes

1. Overview

Mechanics & Materials covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IGCSE examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Scalars and vectors: distance vs displacement, speed vs velocity
  • Equations of motion: s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as
  • Newton's laws of motion and free-body diagrams
  • Moments, torque and principle of moments
  • Hooke's law: F = kx, elastic potential energy = ½kx²
  • Stress, strain and Young modulus: E = stress/strain
  • Density and pressure: p = hρg for fluids

3. Important Formulas and Relationships

4. Common Exam Questions

Examiners frequently test:

  • Definitions and explanations of key terms
  • Application of concepts to novel situations
  • Analysis and evaluation of theories and models
  • Comparison between different approaches or periods
