Biological Molecules

Last-Minute Revision

Key Points

  • Carbohydrates: C, H, O. Monosaccharides → disaccharides (condensation, glycosidic bond) → polysaccharides.
  • Starch (amylose + amylopectin) = plant energy store. Glycogen = animal energy store. Cellulose = plant structural; β-glucose, microfibrils.
  • Lipids: triglycerides (glycerol + 3 fatty acids, ester bonds); phospholipids (bilayer); saturated vs. unsaturated fatty acids.
  • Proteins: amino acids linked by peptide bonds. Primary → secondary (α-helix/β-sheet) → tertiary (R group interactions) → quaternary (multiple chains).
  • Tests: Benedict's (reducing sugars), iodine (starch), Biuret (protein), emulsion (lipid).
  • Water: polar, hydrogen bonding, high specific heat capacity, solvent, cohesion/adhesion.
  • Haemoglobin: 4 subunits, haem with Fe2+, sickle cell = point mutation (valine replaces glutamic acid).
  • Collagen: triple helix, fibrous, high tensile strength.
  • α-glucose vs. β-glucose: differs at C1; determines glycosidic bond geometry.

Exam tip: Always name the type of bond: glycosidic (carbohydrates), ester (lipids), peptide (proteins), hydrogen (between bases in DNA or cellulose microfibrils). Use "condensation" not "dehydration synthesis" in Cambridge exams.
