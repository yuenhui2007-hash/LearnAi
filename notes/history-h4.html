The Cold War in Asia, 1945–1991

history-h4

📘 IGCSE Content

Chinese Civil War & Communist Victory

  • 1945–49: Civil war resumes after Japanese defeat
  • CCP victory (1949): Mao Zedong proclaims People's Republic; Chiang Kai-shek retreats to Taiwan
  • Reasons for CCP victory: Peasant support, effective guerrilla warfare, KMT corruption

Korean War (1950–53)

  • June 1950: North Korea (Kim Il-sung) invades South
  • UN intervention: Led by US General MacArthur
  • Chinese intervention (Oct 1950): 300,000 "volunteers" push UN back to 38th parallel
  • Armistice (1953): Korea remains divided at 38th parallel

Vietnam War

  • 1954: Geneva Conference divides Vietnam at 17th parallel
  • US escalation: Gulf of Tonkin incident (1964) → full ground war
  • Tet Offensive (1968): Military defeat but psychological victory for Viet Cong
  • US withdrawal (1973): Paris Peace Accords
  • Fall of Saigon (1975): Communist victory; reunification

📗 AS Level Content

Containment in Asia

  • Domino Theory: If one country falls to communism, others will follow
  • SEATO (1954): Southeast Asia Treaty Organisation
  • Cost of containment: 58,000 US deaths in Vietnam; $150bn spent

Sino-Soviet Split

  • Ideological differences: Mao's revolutionary fervour vs Khrushchev's peaceful coexistence
  • 1960: Soviet advisors withdrawn from China
  • 1969: Border clashes — potential for Sino-US rapprochement

📙 A2 Level Content

US-China Rapprochement

  • 1971: Kissinger secret visit; Ping-Pong diplomacy
  • 1972: Nixon visits China; Shanghai Communiqué
  • Strategic significance: Triangular diplomacy — US plays China against USSR

Why Communism Succeeded in Asia

  • Anti-colonialism: Communism linked to national liberation
  • Peasant support: Land reform promises
  • US failures: Supported unpopular regimes (Diem, Chiang)

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1949: Communist victory in China; Mao proclaims PRC
  • 1950–53: Korean War — divided at 38th parallel
  • 1954: Vietnam divided at 17th parallel
  • 1964–73: US Vietnam War escalation and withdrawal
  • 1975: Fall of Saigon — communist victory
  • 1972: Nixon visits China — Sino-US rapprochement
  • Domino Theory: Justification for US intervention
