International History, 1945–1991

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • UN (1945): Security Council with veto; peacekeeping successes (Suez, Korea) but paralysed by superpower rivalry.
  • Decolonisation: India/Pakistan 1947; Ghana 1957; "Year of Africa" 1960; Algeria 1962. NAM founded 1961 (Tito, Nasser, Nehru, Nkrumah).
  • Middle East: 1948 Arab-Israeli War; 1956 Suez Crisis; 1967 Six-Day War (Israel takes Sinai, West Bank, Golan, Gaza); 1973 Yom Kippur War → oil crisis; 1978 Camp David Accords.
  • Cuba: Castro 1959; Bay of Pigs 1961; Missile Crisis Oct 1962 — blockade, secret Turkey deal, missiles withdrawn.
  • Iran 1979: Islamic Revolution; US hostage crisis. Iran-Iraq War 1980–88.
  • Arms control: Test Ban 1963; NPT 1968; SALT I 1972; ABM Treaty; INF 1987.
  • Exam Tip: Separate local from superpower causes. Know the sequence: 1948 → 1956 → 1962 → 1967 → 1973 → 1979. Cuban Missile Crisis had public and secret dimensions.
