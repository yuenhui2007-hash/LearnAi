9990 — Attachment, Cognitive Development, and Aging

Last-Minute Summary

  • Bowlby: attachment = critical period, monotropy, internal working model, secure base
  • Ainsworth: Secure (B), Avoidant (A), Resistant (C), Disorganised (D)
  • Piaget's stages: Sensorimotor → Pre-operational → Concrete Operational → Formal Operational
  • Piaget: schema, assimilation, accommodation, equilibration
  • Vygotsky: social interaction drives development; ZPD, scaffolding, internal speech
  • Deprivation = attachment lost; Privation = no attachment formed
  • Aging: fluid intelligence declines; crystallised intelligence stable
  • Evaluate all theories: methodology, sample bias, cultural bias, alternative explanations
