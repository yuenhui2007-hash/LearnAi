Unit 1 — Basic Economic Ideas & Resource Allocation (AS)

  • Scarcity, Choice & Opportunity Cost: Fundamental economic problem; production possibility curves (PPC)
  • Positive vs Normative: Facts vs opinions; role in economic analysis
  • Economic Systems: Free market, planned, mixed — features, advantages, disadvantages
  • Factors of Production: Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise — rewards and mobility
  • Specialisation & Division of Labour: Benefits (productivity) vs drawbacks (boredom, inflexibility)
  • Resource Allocation: Market mechanism (price signals) vs government planning

Unit 2 — The Price System & Theory of the Firm (AS)

  • Demand: Law of demand; determinants (income, substitutes, complements, tastes, expectations)
  • Supply: Law of supply; determinants (input prices, technology, taxes/subsidies, number of firms)
  • Market Equilibrium: Price where Qd = Qs; excess demand and excess supply
  • Elasticities: PED (price elasticity), YED (income), XED (cross), PES (supply) — formulas, determinants, implications
  • Consumer & Producer Surplus: Areas on supply-demand diagrams; welfare analysis
  • Theory of the Firm: Costs (TFC, TVC, TC, AFC, AVC, AC, MC); Revenue (TR, AR, MR); Profit maximisation (MC = MR)
  • Market Structures: Perfect competition, Monopoly, Monopolistic competition, Oligopoly — features, efficiency, diagrams
  • Market Failure: Externalities (negative/positive), Public goods, Merit/demerit goods, Market power, Inequality
  • Government Responses: Taxes, subsidies, regulation, provision, tradable permits, information

Unit 3 — Government Microeconomic Intervention (AS)

  • Price Controls: Maximum prices (ceilings → shortages) and Minimum prices (floors → surpluses)
  • Taxes: Specific vs ad valorem; incidence depends on elasticity; deadweight loss
  • Subsidies: Effects on consumers and producers; distribution depends on elasticity
  • Transfer Payments: Means-tested, universal, conditional
  • Direct Provision: Public goods, merit goods, natural monopolies
  • Regulation: Price regulation, quality standards, quantity restrictions, information requirements
  • Competition Policy: Anti-trust, merger control, price fixing prohibition
  • Privatisation vs Nationalisation: Arguments for and against each

Unit 4 — The Macroeconomy (A2)

  • National Income: GDP, GNP, NNP; nominal vs real; GDP per capita; three methods of measurement
  • Circular Flow: Injections (I, G, X) vs Leakages (S, T, M); equilibrium condition
  • Aggregate Demand: AD = C + I + G + (X − M); determinants of each component
  • Aggregate Supply: SRAS (upward sloping) vs LRAS (vertical at Yf); shifts in both
  • Economic Growth: Actual vs potential; costs and benefits; sources (capital, labour, technology, human capital)
  • Unemployment: Types (frictional, structural, cyclical, seasonal, technological); natural rate; costs
  • Inflation: Demand-pull, cost-push, built-in, monetary; costs (menu, shoe-leather, redistribution, uncertainty)
  • Balance of Payments: Current account, capital account, financial account; causes and consequences of deficits
  • Exchange Rates: Fixed, floating, managed float; factors affecting; J-curve effect

Unit 5 — Government Macroeconomic Intervention (A2)

  • Fiscal Policy: Government spending and taxation; expansionary vs contractionary; automatic stabilisers; budget balance; national debt
  • Monetary Policy: Interest rates, quantitative easing, reserve requirements, open market operations; transmission mechanism
  • Supply-Side Policy: Labour market reforms, deregulation, R&D incentives, infrastructure; evaluation
  • Phillips Curve: Short-run (inverse relationship) vs Long-run (vertical at natural rate); expectations-augmented
  • International Trade: Comparative advantage; terms of trade; protectionism vs free trade
  • Development Economics: Measuring development (HDI, Gini); barriers; strategies (export-oriented, import substitution, aid, microfinance)
