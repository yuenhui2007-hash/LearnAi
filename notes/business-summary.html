💼 Business (9609)

Complete A-Level Syllabus Notes — Summarised

📊 Quick Formula Sheet

Market Share = (Firm's Sales / Total Market Sales) × 100
Market Growth = [(New − Old) / Old] × 100
Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100
Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100
ROCE = (Operating Profit / Capital Employed) × 100
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
Acid Test = (CA − Stock) / CL
Break-even = Fixed Costs / Contribution per unit
Contribution = Selling Price − Variable Cost
NPV = Σ [Cash Flow / (1+r)ⁿ]
Labour Productivity = Output / Number of Employees
Capacity Utilisation = (Actual Output / Max Capacity) × 100

Unit 1 — Business and its Environment

  • Scarcity & Choice: Unlimited wants vs limited resources; opportunity cost
  • Economic Systems: Free market, planned, mixed — features & evaluation
  • Business Objectives: Profit max, sales max, survival, CSR, satisficing
  • Stakeholders: Internal (employees, managers, shareholders) vs External (government, suppliers, community)
  • Business Classification: By sector (primary, secondary, tertiary), size, ownership (sole trader, partnership, Ltd, Plc)
  • External Environment: PESTLE analysis — Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental
  • Internal Growth: Organic growth through reinvesting profits
  • External Growth: Mergers (horizontal, vertical, conglomerate) & takeovers

Unit 2 — Marketing

  • Marketing Mix (4Ps): Product, Price, Place, Promotion (+ People, Process, Physical Evidence for services)
  • Market Research: Primary (surveys, focus groups) vs Secondary (existing data); Qualitative vs Quantitative
  • Segmentation: Demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural
  • Targeting: Niche vs Mass marketing; Differentiated vs Undifferentiated
  • Positioning: Perceptual maps; Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
  • Product Life Cycle: Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline; strategies at each stage
  • Boston Matrix: Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs
  • Pricing Strategies: Penetration, skimming, cost-plus, competitive, psychological, promotional
  • Promotion: Above-the-line (TV, radio, print) vs Below-the-line (sales promo, direct mail, social media)
  • Digital Marketing: SEO, PPC, content marketing, influencer marketing, email marketing
  • E-commerce: Benefits (global reach, 24/7, lower costs) vs Drawbacks (security, lack of personal touch)

Unit 3 — Finance and Accounting

  • Sources of Finance: Internal (retained profit, sale of assets) vs External (share capital, loans, overdrafts, debentures, venture capital, crowdfunding)
  • Short vs Long-term: Working capital needs vs capital investment
  • Cash Flow: Cash flow forecasting; causes of cash flow problems; solutions (debt factoring, sale and leaseback, overdrafts)
  • Working Capital: Current assets − Current liabilities; management of inventory, receivables, payables
  • Income Statement: Revenue − Cost of Sales = Gross Profit; Gross Profit − Expenses = Operating Profit; Operating Profit − Interest & Tax = Net Profit
  • Statement of Financial Position: Assets (NCA + CA) = Equity + Liabilities (NCL + CL)
  • Ratio Analysis: Profitability (GPM, NPM, ROCE), Liquidity (Current, Acid Test), Efficiency (Inventory turnover, Receivables days, Payables days), Gearing (Debt/Equity, Interest cover), Investor (EPS, Dividend yield, P/E)
  • Investment Appraisal: Payback, ARR, NPV (with discount factors), IRR
  • Break-even Analysis: Break-even output, margin of safety, contribution, C/S ratio
  • Budgets: Purposes (planning, control, motivation, coordination); variance analysis (favourable vs adverse)

Unit 4 — Operations Management

  • Operations Role: Transforming inputs into outputs; adding value
  • Methods of Production: Job, Batch, Flow (mass & continuous); cell production, lean production
  • Lean Production: JIT (Just in Time), Kaizen (continuous improvement), TQM (Total Quality Management), Kanban, Andon
  • Quality: Quality control (inspection) vs Quality assurance (systems) vs TQM (culture)
  • Capacity: Capacity utilisation; ways to increase capacity (overtime, subcontracting, new premises)
  • Stock Control: Buffer stock, reorder level, reorder quantity, lead time; JIT pros & cons
  • Location: Factors (market access, labour, costs, infrastructure, government incentives)
  • Scale: Internal economies of scale (technical, purchasing, managerial, financial, marketing, risk-bearing); Diseconomies of scale
  • Innovation & R&D: Product vs Process innovation; importance of R&D for competitiveness
  • Technology: CAD, CAM, CIM, AI, automation, robotics
  • Sustainability: Triple bottom line (People, Planet, Profit); circular economy; CSR

Unit 5 — People in Organisations

  • Organisational Structure: Hierarchical (tall) vs Flat vs Matrix; span of control, chain of command, delegation, centralisation vs decentralisation
  • Motivation Theories: Taylor (scientific management), Maslow (hierarchy of needs), Herzberg (two-factor theory: motivators & hygiene factors), Mayo (human relations), Adams (equity theory), Vroom (expectancy theory)
  • Leadership Styles: Autocratic, Democratic, Laissez-faire, Paternalistic, Situational (Tannenbaum & Schmidt, Blake & Mouton)
  • Recruitment: Job analysis, job description, person specification; internal vs external recruitment
  • Selection: Application forms, CVs, interviews, assessment centres, psychometric tests
  • Training: Induction, on-the-job, off-the-job; benefits (productivity, motivation, retention)
  • Remuneration: Time-based, piece-rate, commission, salary, profit-sharing, fringe benefits
  • Employment Legislation: Health & safety, equal opportunities, minimum wage, working time directives
  • Communication: Formal vs informal; vertical, horizontal, diagonal; barriers and solutions
  • Industrial Relations: Trade unions, collective bargaining, conciliation, arbitration, industrial action

Unit 6 — Strategic Management (A2)

  • Strategic Analysis: SWOT (internal Strengths/Weaknesses, external Opportunities/Threats); PESTLE; Porter's Five Forces; Boston Matrix; Ansoff Matrix
  • Strategic Choice: Ansoff Matrix (Market Penetration, Product Development, Market Development, Diversification); Investment appraisal; Decision trees (expected values)
  • Strategic Implementation: Managing change (Lewin's model: unfreeze → change → refreeze); Kotter's 8-step change model; overcoming resistance
  • Corporate Culture: Power, Role, Task, Person cultures (Handy); importance for strategy
  • Globalisation: Drivers (technology, trade liberalisation, MNCs); impact on businesses; glocalisation
  • International Trade: Free trade vs Protectionism (tariffs, quotas, subsidies, embargoes); WTO, trading blocs (EU, ASEAN, USMCA)
  • Exchange Rates: Appreciation vs Depreciation; impact on exporters and importers
  • Ethics & CSR: Ethical decision-making; stakeholder vs shareholder approach; sustainability reporting
  • Contingency Planning: Risk assessment; crisis management; business continuity
