Double Entry

0452 / 9706 — The Debit-Credit System

IGCSE Content

The Golden Rule

Double entry means every transaction has TWO equal and opposite effects. For every debit there must be an equal credit. The total debits always equal total credits.

DEAD CLIC
Debits increase: Expenses, Assets, Drawings
Credits increase: Liabilities, Income, Capital

Rules for Different Account Types

  • Assets: Debit = Increase, Credit = Decrease
  • Liabilities: Debit = Decrease, Credit = Increase
  • Capital: Debit = Decrease (drawings), Credit = Increase
  • Income: Debit = Decrease, Credit = Increase
  • Expenses: Debit = Increase, Credit = Decrease

Common Transactions — Journal Entries

1. Started business with cash:

Dr Cash $10,000
Cr Capital $10,000

2. Bought inventory on credit:

Dr Purchases $4,000
Cr Trade Payables $4,000

3. Sold goods for cash:

Dr Cash $2,500
Cr Sales $2,500

4. Paid rent by cheque:

Dr Rent Expense $800
Cr Bank $800

5. Owner withdrew cash:

Dr Drawings $500
Cr Cash $500

AS Level Content

Balancing Accounts

At period end, accounts are balanced to find the closing balance:

  1. Total both sides
  2. Insert the balancing figure on the smaller side (this is the "balance c/d")
  3. The totals of both sides now equal — write them on the same line
  4. Bring the balance down ("b/d") on the opposite side to start the next period

Discounts

  • Trade discount: Deducted at point of sale (e.g., bulk discount) — not recorded in books; deducted before entry
  • Cash discount: Early payment incentive — recorded as Discount Received (income) or Discount Allowed (expense)
Discount Allowed (to customers): Dr Discount Allowed, Cr Trade Receivables
Discount Received (from suppliers): Dr Trade Payables, Cr Discount Received

A2 Level Content

Complex Adjustments

  • Accruals: Expenses incurred but not yet paid — Dr Expense, Cr Accruals (liability)
  • Prepayments: Expenses paid in advance — Dr Prepayment (asset), Cr Expense
  • Irrecoverable debts: Debt known to be uncollectable — Dr Irrecoverable Debts Expense, Cr Trade Receivables
  • Allowance for doubtful debts: Estimate of future bad debts — Dr Doubtful Debts Expense, Cr Allowance for Doubtful Debts (contra asset)

Stock Valuation (IAS 2)

Inventory must be valued at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV):

NRV = Expected Selling Price − Estimated Costs of Completion/Sale
If NRV < Cost, write down to NRV: Dr Cost of Sales, Cr Inventory

Last-Minute Summary

  • DEAD CLIC: Debits increase Expenses, Assets, Drawings; Credits increase Liabilities, Income, Capital
  • Every transaction = at least one debit and one credit of equal value
  • Assets: Debit ↑ Credit ↓ | Liabilities: Debit ↓ Credit ↑ | Capital: Debit ↓ Credit ↑
  • Income: Credit ↑ | Expenses: Debit ↑
  • Balance c/d on the smaller side; balance b/d on the opposite side
  • Trade discount: not recorded; Cash discount: Discount Allowed (expense) / Discount Received (income)
  • Accruals: owe but not paid; Prepayments: paid but not yet used
  • Irrecoverable debt: remove from receivables; Allowance: estimate future bad debts
  • Inventory at lower of cost and NRV
