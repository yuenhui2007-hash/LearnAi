Britain, 1918–1951

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1918: "Khaki election"; Lloyd George coalition. 1921: Anglo-Irish Treaty.
  • 1922: Lloyd George falls; Conservative dominance. 1924: First Labour government.
  • 1926: General Strike (4–12 May); miners locked out. 1929: Labour largest party.
  • 1931: National Government; gold standard abandoned. 1936: Jarrow March; Abdication crisis.
  • 1938: Munich; rearmament accelerates. 1939: Guarantee to Poland; conscription.
  • 1940: Churchill PM (10 May); Battle of Britain; Blitz.
  • 1942: El Alamein; Beveridge Report. 1945: Labour landslide (July).
  • Attlee 1945–51: NHS (1948), nationalisation, welfare state, India/Pakistan (1947).
  • Exam Tip: Key dates: 1926 (General Strike), 1931 (National Gov), 1938 (Munich), 1940 (Churchill), 1945 (Labour landslide). Track economy, society, and empire alongside politics.
