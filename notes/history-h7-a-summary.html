The French Revolution, 1774–1794

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Causes: Debt, tax inequality, Three Estates, Enlightenment ideas, bad harvests.
  • 1789: Estates-General (May); National Assembly (17 June); Tennis Court Oath (20 June); Bastille (14 July); Great Fear; August Decrees; Declaration of Rights (26 Aug).
  • 1791: Constitution (constitutional monarchy); Civil Constitution of the Clergy.
  • 1792: War (April); Brunswick Manifesto; Tuileries stormed (10 Aug); Republic (Sept); Louis XVI executed (21 Jan 1793).
  • 1793–94: Committee of Public Safety; Terror; Maximum; dechristianisation; 17,000–40,000 executed.
  • 27 July 1794: Thermidor — Robespierre executed; Terror ends.
  • Historiography: Marxist (Lefebvre — bourgeois revolution) vs Revisionist (Cobban/Furet — political not social) vs Cultural (language/ideology).
  • Exam Tip: Know the progression: reform → constitutional monarchy → republic → terror → thermidor. Link each phase to causes (war, economy, ideology).
