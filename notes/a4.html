Ledger Accounts

0452 / 9706 — General, Sales, and Purchases Ledgers

IGCSE Content

The Three Ledgers

  • General Ledger (Nominal Ledger): Contains all accounts except individual customer and supplier accounts. Includes asset, liability, capital, income, and expense accounts. Used to prepare the trial balance and financial statements.
  • Sales Ledger (Debtors Ledger): Contains individual accounts for each credit customer. Tracks how much each customer owes.
  • Purchases Ledger (Creditors Ledger): Contains individual accounts for each credit supplier. Tracks how much is owed to each supplier.

General Ledger Account Types

  • Real (Asset) Accounts: Cash, Bank, Equipment, Buildings, Inventory, Vehicles
  • Personal Accounts (in General Ledger): Sales Ledger Control, Purchases Ledger Control
  • Nominal Accounts: Sales, Purchases, Rent, Wages, Insurance, Discount Allowed/Received

Posting from Books of Prime Entry

Sales Day Book → Dr Sales Ledger Control (GL), Cr Sales (GL)
Purchases Day Book → Dr Purchases (GL), Cr Purchases Ledger Control (GL)
Sales Returns → Dr Sales Returns (GL), Cr Sales Ledger Control (GL)
Purchases Returns → Dr Purchases Ledger Control (GL), Cr Purchases Returns (GL)
Cash Book Receipts → Dr Cash/Bank (GL), Cr relevant accounts
Cash Book Payments → Dr relevant accounts, Cr Cash/Bank (GL)

AS Level Content

Control Accounts

Control accounts in the General Ledger mirror the total of all individual accounts in the subsidiary ledger:

  • Sales Ledger Control: Total of all customer balances (should equal sum of sales ledger)
  • Purchases Ledger Control: Total of all supplier balances (should equal sum of purchases ledger)
Sales Ledger Control Account:
Dr: Balance b/d, Credit sales (from SDB), Dishonoured cheques, Interest charged
Cr: Cash received, Sales returns, Discount allowed, Irrecoverable debts, Set-offs (contra), Balance c/d

Purchases Ledger Control Account:
Dr: Cash paid, Purchases returns, Discount received, Set-offs (contra), Balance c/d
Cr: Balance b/d, Credit purchases (from PDB), Interest charged

Reconciling Control Accounts

  • Total of individual balances in sales/purchases ledger should equal the control account balance
  • Differences indicate errors in either the individual accounts or the control account
  • Common errors: omission, commission, principle, original entry, reversal, compensating

A2 Level Content

Suspense Accounts and Error Correction

When the trial balance doesn't agree, a suspense account is opened with the difference. Errors are found and corrected through the journal:

  • Error of omission: Transaction not recorded at all → Dr/Cr correct accounts
  • Error of commission: Wrong account but correct type (e.g., wrong customer) → transfer between accounts
  • Error of principle: Wrong category (e.g., expense recorded as asset) → reverse and correct
  • Error of original entry: Wrong amount → adjust for difference
  • Error of reversal: Debits/credits swapped → double the amount to correct
  • Compensating errors: Two errors that cancel out — both must be corrected

Effect on Profit and Net Assets

For each error, determine:

  • Does it affect profit? (if income or expense is wrong)
  • Does it affect net assets? (if asset, liability, or capital is wrong)
  • Has the suspense account been used?
