Angles, Parallel Lines & Polygons

geometry-g2

📘 IGCSE Content

Angle Types

  • Acute: 0° < θ < 90°
  • Right: θ = 90°
  • Obtuse: 90° < θ < 180°
  • Straight: θ = 180°
  • Reflex: 180° < θ < 360°

Angle Relationships

  • Vertically opposite angles: Equal
  • Angles on a straight line: Sum to 180°
  • Angles around a point: Sum to 360°

Parallel Lines

  • Corresponding angles: Equal
  • Alternate angles: Equal
  • Co-interior angles: Sum to 180°

Polygons

  • Sum of interior angles: (n − 2) × 180°
  • Sum of exterior angles: Always 360°
  • Regular polygon interior angle: (n − 2) × 180° / n

📗 AS Level Content

Polygon Properties

  • Convex vs concave: All interior angles < 180° vs at least one > 180°
  • Number of diagonals: n(n − 3)/2

Worked Example

Find the interior angle of a regular decagon.
n = 10
Interior angle = (10 − 2) × 180° / 10 = 8 × 18° = 144°

📙 A2 Level Content

Tessellations

  • Regular tessellations: Only triangles, squares, hexagons
  • Semi-regular: Combination of regular polygons

📐 Worked Example

Question: Find the interior angle of a regular decagon.

Solution:
n = 10
Interior angle = (n − 2) × 180° / n
= (10 − 2) × 180° / 10 = 8 × 18° = 144°
Answer: 144°
Confusing interior and exterior angles. Exterior angles of ANY polygon always sum to 360°.
For regular polygons: interior + exterior = 180°. Use this to check your answers.
