Human Body Systems covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IGCSE examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

  • Circulatory system: double circulatory system
  • Immune system: white blood cells, antibodies, antigens
  • Reproductive system: gametes, fertilisation, menstrual cycle
