Trigonometry

Basic Identities

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
tan θ = sin θcos θ

Compound Angle Formulae

sin(A ± B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B
cos(A ± B) = cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B
tan(A ± B) = tan A ± tan B1 ∓ tan A tan B

Double Angle Formulae

sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A
cos 2A = cos²A − sin²A = 2cos²A − 1 = 1 − 2sin²A
tan 2A = 2 tan A1 − tan²A
Worked Example

Given sin A = 3/5 and A is acute, find sin 2A and cos 2A.

cos A = 4/5

sin 2A = 2 × 35 × 45 = 2425

cos 2A = 1625 − 925 = 725

Worked Example — Solving Equations

Solve: 2 sin²x + cos x − 1 = 0 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π.

2(1−cos²x) + cos x − 1 = 0 → 2cos²x − cos x − 1 = 0

(2cos x + 1)(cos x − 1) = 0

cos x = −1/2 or cos x = 1

x = 0, 2π3, 4π3, 2π

Key Point: When solving trig equations, use identities to express everything in terms of one trig function. Always check all solutions in the given interval.

📝 Quick Summary

  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
  • sin(A±B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B
  • cos(A±B) = cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B
  • sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A
  • cos 2A = cos²A − sin²A = 2cos²A − 1 = 1 − 2sin²A

💡 Exam Tips

  • Learn the compound angle and double angle formulae by heart.
  • Use the identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 to convert between sin and cos.
  • When solving equations, find all solutions in the given interval.
  • Sketch the graph to check you have found all solutions.
