Basic Economic Ideas

Syllabus reference: Cambridge 9708 / 0455 — Topic 1

The Fundamental Economic Problem

Scarcity — unlimited wants vs limited resources. This forces choices, and choices involve opportunity cost: the next best alternative foregone.

Factors of Production

  • Land — natural resources (rent)
  • Labour — human effort (wages)
  • Capital — man-made goods used to produce other goods (interest)
  • Enterprise — risk-taking and organisation (profit)

Production Possibility Curve (PPC)

A curve showing the maximum combinations of two goods that can be produced with existing resources.

  • Points on the curve — efficient, full employment
  • Points inside the curve — inefficient, unemployment
  • Points outside — unattainable with current resources
  • Outward shift — economic growth (more resources or better technology)

Positive vs Normative Statements

  • Positive — objective, can be tested/falsified ("Unemployment is 5%")
  • Normative — subjective, involves value judgements ("Unemployment is too high")

Exam Tips

  • Always mention opportunity cost when discussing choices.
  • Draw PPC diagrams with clear labels and shifts.
  • Distinguish between a movement along the PPC and a shift of the PPC.
