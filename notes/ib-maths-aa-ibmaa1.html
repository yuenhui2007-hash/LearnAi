Number & Algebra

IB DP Mathematics: Analysis & Approaches HL/SL — Topic 1

Overview

Number and Algebra (Topic 1) forms the foundation of mathematical reasoning in IB Mathematics AA. It includes sequences and series, exponents, logarithms, the binomial theorem, proof techniques, and complex numbers for HL. This topic appears across all papers and underpins much of calculus and functions.

1.1 Sequences & Series

Arithmetic Sequences

uₙ = u₁ + (n − 1)d
S�� = n/2 (2u₁ + (n − 1)d) = n/2 (u₁ + uₙ)

Geometric Sequences

uₙ = u₁ r^(n−1)
S�� = u₁(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r) for r ≠ 1
S_∞ = u₁/(1 − r) for |r| < 1

Worked Example 1: Geometric Series

Question: Find the sum to infinity of 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 + ...

Solution:

u₁ = 8, r = 4/8 = 0.5
S_∞ = 8/(1 − 0.5) = 16

1.2 Exponents & Logarithms

a^m × a^n = a^(m+n)
a^m / a^n = a^(m−n)
(a^m)^n = a^(mn)
a^0 = 1
logₐ(xy) = logₐx + logₐy
logₐ(x/y) = logₐx − logₐy
logₐ(x^n) = n logₐx
logₐa = 1, logₐ1 = 0
Change of base: logₐb = log_c b / log_c a

Worked Example 2: Solving Exponential Equations

Question: Solve 3^(2x) = 7.

Solution:

Take ln of both sides: 2x ln 3 = ln 7
x = ln 7 / (2 ln 3) = 0.885 (3 sf)

1.3 Binomial Theorem

(a + b)ⁿ = Σ (n choose r) a^(n−r) b^r for r = 0 to n
where (n choose r) = n! / (r!(n−r)!)

Worked Example 3: Binomial Expansion

Question: Find the coefficient of x³ in the expansion of (2x + 1)⁵.

Solution:

General term: (5 choose r)(2x)^(5−r)(1)^r
For x³: 5−r = 3 → r = 2
Coefficient = (5 choose 2)(2)³ = 10 × 8 = 80

1.4 Proof Techniques [HL only]

  • Proof by deduction: direct logical argument from known facts.
  • Proof by exhaustion: checking all possible cases.
  • Proof by contradiction: assume the opposite is true and show this leads to a contradiction.
  • Proof by induction: prove for n=1, assume for n=k, prove for n=k+1.

HL Exam Focus

May 2024 HL Paper 1: Proof by induction for a summation formula.

1.5 Complex Numbers [HL only]

z = a + bi, where i² = −1
Modulus: |z| = √(a² + b²)
Argument: arg(z) = arctan(b/a) (adjusted for quadrant)
Polar form: z = r(cis θ) = r(cos θ + i sin θ)
De Moivre's theorem: (r cis θ)ⁿ = rⁿ cis(nθ)

Common Misconceptions

  • Geometric series: S_∞ only converges if |r| < 1.
  • Logarithms: log(a + b) ≠ log a + log b. Only multiplication inside the log becomes addition outside.
  • Binomial: The power of the first term decreases; the power of the second term increases.
  • Proof by induction: Students often forget to prove the n=1 base case.

Exam Tips

  • Read each question carefully and identify the command term before answering.
  • Show all working — method marks are generous in IB Mathematics.
  • Give exact answers unless asked for decimal approximations.
  • For proof questions, write each step clearly and justify transitions.
  • Check that |r| < 1 before using S_∞ formula.
