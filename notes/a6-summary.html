0452 / 9706 — Income Statement & Statement of Financial Position

Last-Minute Summary

  • Income Statement: Revenue − Cost of Sales = Gross Profit; Gross Profit − Expenses + Other Income = Net Profit
  • SOFP: Non-Current Assets + Current Assets = Total Assets; Capital + Liabilities = Total Equity & Liabilities
  • Cost of Sales = Opening Inventory + Net Purchases − Closing Inventory
  • Capital at end = Opening Capital + Profit − Drawings + Capital Introduced
  • Current assets: inventory, receivables (less allowance), prepayments, cash
  • Current liabilities: payables, accruals, bank overdraft, short-term loans
  • Adjustments: accruals (+expense/+liability), prepayments (−expense/+asset), depreciation (+expense/−NCA)
  • Inventory valued at lower of cost and NRV
  • Irrecoverable debts = expense; allowance for doubtful debts = contra-asset
  • Revenue recognised when control transfers to customer (IFRS 15)
