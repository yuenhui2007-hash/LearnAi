Enzymes

Last-Minute Revision · 0610 (IGCSE)

Last-Minute Revision

Key Points

  • Enzymes are globular protein catalysts; specific due to active site shape.
  • Lock-and-key (rigid active site) → induced fit (flexible active site changes shape on substrate binding).
  • Factors: temperature (optimum ~37°C; denatured above), pH (optimum varies), substrate concentration, enzyme concentration.
  • Rate = change / time. Use initial rate from steepest part of progress curve.
  • Competitive inhibition: inhibitor competes for active site; overcome by increasing [S].
  • Non-competitive inhibition: inhibitor binds allosteric site; cannot be overcome by increasing [S].
  • Immobilised enzymes: reusable, stable, product not contaminated, continuous processing.
  • Allosteric regulation / end-product inhibition controls metabolic pathways.
  • Key enzymes: PFK (glycolysis), rubisco (Calvin cycle), ATP synthase (chemiosmosis).

Exam tip: Distinguish denaturation (loss of 3D structure, usually irreversible) from inhibition (reversible blocking). Draw graphs with labelled axes: rate (y) vs. temperature/pH/substrate concentration (x). Show optimum peak for temperature/pH; plateau for substrate concentration.
