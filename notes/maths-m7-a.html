Basic Rules

Tangents and Normals

GradientEquation
Tangentm = f'(a)y − f(a) = f'(a)(x − a)
Normalm = −1/f'(a)y − f(a) = −1/f'(a)(x − a)
Worked Example

Find tangent to y = x² at x = 3.

dy/dx = 2x → at x=3, m = 6. Point: (3, 9)

y − 9 = 6(x − 3) → y = 6x − 9

Stationary Points

TypeCondition
Maximumf'(x) = 0 and f''(x) < 0
Minimumf'(x) = 0 and f''(x) > 0
Point of inflexionf'(x) = 0 and f''(x) = 0 (check further)
Chain rule: dy/dx = dy/du × du/dx

📝 Quick Summary

  • Power rule: d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹
  • Chain rule: dy/dx = dy/du × du/dx
  • Stationary point: f'(x) = 0
  • Maximum: f''(x) < 0; Minimum: f''(x) > 0
  • Tangent gradient = f'(a); Normal gradient = −1/f'(a)

💡 Exam Tips

  • Always simplify before differentiating
  • For stationary points, show f'(x) = 0 and use second derivative
  • Don't forget the constant multiple rule
  • Connected rates: identify what you need and what you're given
