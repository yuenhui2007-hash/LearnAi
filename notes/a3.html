Books of Prime Entry

0452 / 9706 — Day Books, Cash Books, and Journals

IGCSE Content

The Six Books of Prime Entry

  • Sales Day Book (SDB): Records credit sales only. Total posted: Dr Sales Ledger Control, Cr Sales.
  • Purchases Day Book (PDB): Records credit purchases only. Total posted: Dr Purchases, Cr Purchases Ledger Control.
  • Sales Returns Day Book (SRDB): Records returns IN from credit customers. Total posted: Dr Sales Returns, Cr Sales Ledger Control.
  • Purchases Returns Day Book (PRDB): Records returns OUT to credit suppliers. Total posted: Dr Purchases Ledger Control, Cr Purchases Returns.
  • Cash Book: Records ALL cash and bank transactions. Acts as both a book of prime entry AND a ledger account for cash/bank.
  • General Journal (Journal): Records transactions not in other books — opening entries, adjustments, corrections, depreciation, purchases/sales of non-current assets on credit.

Cash Book Format

Two-column Cash Book:
Dr side: Date | Details | Disc. | Cash | Bank
Cr side: Date | Details | Disc. | Cash | Bank

Discount columns are NOT part of double entry — totals posted to Discount Allowed/Received accounts.

AS Level Content

Three-Column Cash Book

Includes discount columns on both sides to track cash discounts:

  • Discount Allowed column (Dr side): Discount given to customers for early payment → posted to Discount Allowed (expense)
  • Discount Received column (Cr side): Discount received from suppliers → posted to Discount Received (income)

Petty Cash Book (Imprest System)

A petty cash book records small cash payments:

  • Imprest amount: Fixed float (e.g., $200)
  • Payments recorded with vouchers; receipts kept
  • At period end, total paid out is reimbursed to restore imprest
Petty Cash Reimbursement:
Cash in hand + Total vouchers = Imprest amount
Dr Expense accounts, Cr Cash/Bank (for reimbursement amount)

The Journal — Complex Entries

  • Purchase of non-current asset on credit: Dr Asset, Cr Supplier
  • Depreciation: Dr Depreciation Expense, Cr Accumulated Depreciation
  • Irrecoverable debts: Dr Irrecoverable Debts, Cr Trade Receivables
  • Opening entries: Transfer balances from previous period
  • Correction of errors: Reversing wrong entry + correct entry

A2 Level Content

Trade vs Cash Discounts in Books

  • Trade discount: Deducted before recording — invoice shows net amount only. Never appears in books.
  • Cash discount: Recorded in cash book discount columns. Discount Allowed is an expense; Discount Received is income.

Bank Reconciliation Entries in Cash Book

When preparing bank reconciliation, items found in the bank statement but not in cash book must be recorded:

  • Bank charges/interest: Dr Bank Charges/Interest, Cr Bank
  • Direct debits/standing orders: Dr Expense, Cr Bank
  • Dishonoured cheques: Dr Trade Receivables, Cr Bank (reverse original receipt)
  • Direct credits: Dr Bank, Cr Relevant income account
