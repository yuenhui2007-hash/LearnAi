Detailed Notes

1. Overview

Organic Chemistry covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IGCSE examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Homologous series: alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids
  • IUPAC nomenclature rules and structural formulae
  • Isomerism: structural, stereoisomerism (E/Z, optical)
  • Reactions of alkanes: combustion, substitution
  • Reactions of alkenes: addition, hydrogenation, polymerisation
  • Alcohol reactions: oxidation to aldehydes/ketones/acids
  • Spectroscopy: IR and mass spec for identification

3. Important Formulas and Relationships

Key relationships and equations for Organic Chemistry are essential for problem-solving. Practice applying these to different contexts.

4. Common Exam Questions

Examiners frequently test:

  • Definitions and explanations of key terms
  • Application of concepts to novel situations
  • Analysis and evaluation of theories and models
  • Comparison between different approaches or periods

5. Practical Applications

Connect theoretical knowledge to real-world contexts. Use contemporary examples and case studies where appropriate to demonstrate deeper understanding.
