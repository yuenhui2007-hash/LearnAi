Kinematics

SUVAT Equations

v = u + at
s = ut + 12at²
s = (u + v)2t
v² = u² + 2as
Worked Example

A car accelerates from 5 m/s at 2 m/s² for 4 s. Find final speed and distance.

v = 5 + 2(4) = 13 m/s

s = 5(4) + 12(2)(16) = 20 + 16 = 36 m

Worked Example — Under Gravity

A ball is thrown upwards at 15 m/s. Find max height (g = 10 m/s²).

At max height, v = 0. v² = u² + 2as → 0 = 225 − 20s → s = 11.25 m

Calculus in Kinematics

v = ds/dt; a = dv/dt
s = ∫ v dt; v = ∫ a dt
Worked Example

v = 3t² − 4t + 2, s = 0 when t = 0. Find s at t = 3.

s = t³ − 2t² + 2t + c → c = 0

s(3) = 27 − 18 + 6 = 15 m

Key Point: Choose a positive direction and stick to it. Upward is usually positive for vertical motion, but be consistent with your choice.

📝 Quick Summary

  • v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as
  • v = ds/dt; a = dv/dt
  • Upward positive: a = −g
  • Max height when v = 0

💡 Exam Tips

  • Choose a positive direction and be consistent.
  • For vertical motion under gravity, a = −g if up is positive.
  • At max height, vertical velocity is momentarily zero.
  • When using calculus, distance = ∫|v| and displacement = ∫v.
