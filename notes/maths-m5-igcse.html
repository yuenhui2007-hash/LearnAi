Trigonometric Identities

tan θ ≡ sin θ/cos θ
sin² θ + cos² θ ≡ 1
tan² θ + 1 ≡ sec² θ
1 + cot² θ ≡ cosec² θ
Worked Example

Given sin θ = 3/5 and θ is acute, find cos θ.

sin² θ + cos² θ = 1 → (3/5)² + cos² θ = 1

cos² θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25 → cos θ = 4/5

📝 Quick Summary

  • sin² θ + cos² θ ≡ 1
  • tan θ ≡ sin θ/cos θ
  • sin positive in 1st & 2nd; cos in 1st & 4th; tan in 1st & 3rd
  • CAST diagram for solving equations
  • Period: sin/cos = 360°; tan = 180°

💡 Exam Tips

  • Learn exact values for 30°, 45°, 60°

  • Always check your calculator is in correct mode
  • When solving, find all solutions in the given range
