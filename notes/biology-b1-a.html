Cell Theory

All living organisms are composed of cells. The cell is the basic unit of life. All cells arise from pre-existing cells.

Cell Fractionation

Steps
  1. Homogenise tissue in cold isotonic buffer
  2. Filter through gauze to remove debris
  3. Ultracentrifuge at increasing speeds:
    • Low speed: nuclei pellet
    • Medium speed: mitochondria, lysosomes, peroxisomes
    • High speed: ribosomes

📝 Quick Summary

  • Cell theory: all organisms composed of cells
  • Prokaryotes: no nucleus, 70S ribosomes, no membrane-bound organelles
  • Eukaryotes: true nucleus, 80S ribosomes, membrane-bound organelles
  • Mitochondria = aerobic respiration; Chloroplasts = photosynthesis
  • RER = protein synthesis; SER = lipid synthesis

💡 Exam Tips

  • Always compare structure with function
  • Remember the size difference: prokaryotes 1-10μm, eukaryotes 10-100μm
  • Know the order of ultracentrifugation
