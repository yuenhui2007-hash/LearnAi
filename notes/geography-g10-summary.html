📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Prism volume: cross-section × length
  • Cylinder: V = πr²h, CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πrh + 2πr²
  • Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h, CSA = πrl, l = √(r²+h²)
  • Pyramid: V = (1/3) × base × height
  • Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, SA = 4πr²
  • Frustum: V = (1/3)πh(R²+Rr+r²), CSA = πl(R+r)
  • Similar solids: lengths k, areas k², volumes k³
  • 3D Pythagoras: diagonal = √(a²+b²+c²)
  • Line-plane angle: angle between line and its projection
  • Plane equation: ax + by + cz = d
  • Volume of revolution: V = π ∫ y² dx
  • Great circle distance: Rθ where cos θ uses spherical formula
