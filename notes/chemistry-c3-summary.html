Last-Minute Revision

🎯 Key Points to Remember

  • Mole: n = mass/Mr = V/24 (gas at RTP) = particles/NA
  • Empirical formula: % → mass → moles → ratio → simplest integers
  • Concentration: mol/dm³ = n/V; remember 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³
  • PV = nRT; T in Kelvin; P in Pa; V in m³; R = 8.31
  • Titration: use mole ratios carefully; watch for polyprotic acids
  • Redox titrations: balance electrons; use mole ratios from balanced equations
  • Partition coefficient: ratio of concentrations in two immiscible solvents

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting to convert cm³ to dm³ (÷1000) in concentration calculations
  • Using Celsius instead of Kelvin in gas equations
  • Not balancing equations before doing mole ratio calculations
  • Confusing empirical and molecular formula
  • Forgetting the stoichiometric coefficients in Kc/Kp expressions
