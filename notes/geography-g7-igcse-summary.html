Vectors in Geography — Summary

Geography G7

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Vector magnitude: |**v**| = √(x²+y²+z²)
  • Unit vector: **v̂** = **v**/|**v**|
  • Section formula: **p** = (n**a** + m**b**)/(m+n)
  • Dot product: **a**·**b** = |**a**||**b**|cos θ = a₁b₁+a₂b₂+a₃b₃
  • Cross product: **a**×**b** = |**a**||**b**|sin θ **n̂**
  • Line: **r** = **a** + λ**d**
  • Plane: **r**·**n** = d or ax+by+cz = d
  • Point-line distance: |(**p**−**a**)×**d**|/|**d**|
  • Point-plane distance: |**n**·**p**−d|/|**n**|
  • Scalar triple product: volume of parallelepiped, test for coplanarity
  • Angle between vectors: cos θ = (**a**·**b**)/(|**a**||**b**|)
