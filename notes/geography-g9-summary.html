Coordinate Geometry of Circles & Parabolas — Summary

Geography G9

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Distance: √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
  • Gradient: m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)
  • Perpendicular: m₁ × m₂ = −1
  • Line: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
  • Circle: (x−a)² + (y−b)² = r²
  • General circle: x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0, centre (−g,−f), radius √(g²+f²−c)
  • Parabola vertex: x = −b/(2a)
  • Tangent condition: discriminant = 0
  • Chord of contact: xx₁ + yy₁ = r²
  • Parabola parametric: x=at², y=2at, tangent: ty=x+at²
  • Conic discriminant: B²−4AC < 0 ellipse, = 0 parabola, > 0 hyperbola
