Circles: Theorems & Properties — Summary

Geography G5

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Circumference = 2πr, Area = πr²
  • Angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference
  • Angle in semicircle = 90°
  • Angles in same segment are equal
  • Cyclic quad: opposite angles = 180°
  • Tangent ⊥ radius at point of contact
  • Alternate segment theorem: tangent-chord angle = angle in alternate segment
  • Arc length = rθ, Sector area = ½r²θ (θ in radians)
  • Intersecting chords: PA × PB = PC × PD
  • Tangent-secant: PT² = PA × PB
  • Power of point: OP² − r² = PA × PB
  • Circle equation: (x−a)² + (y−b)² = r²
  • Tangent at (x₁,y₁): xx₁ + yy₁ = r²
  • Tangency condition: c² = r²(1+m²)
