C14: Group 2 & 17 Chemistry

9701 (A-Level) · Last-Minute Revision

📚 Comprehensive Revision Summary

Key Formulae:
• See main notes for detailed equations and calculations
  • Group 2: reactivity increases down group; IE decreases; hydroxide solubility increases; sulfate solubility decreases
  • Thermal stability of carbonates/nitrates increases down group (larger cation polarises anion less)
  • Group 17: reactivity decreases down group; colour darkens; mp/bp increase
  • More reactive halogen displaces less reactive halogen from halide salts
  • Halide test: AgNO₃ + HNO₃; Cl⁻ white, Br⁻ cream, I⁻ yellow
  • Chlorine disproportionates in alkali: cold → ClO⁻; hot → ClO₃⁻
  • HF is weak acid (strong H−F bond); HI is strong reducing agent (weak H−I bond)
  • Chlor-alkali industry: electrolysis of brine produces Cl₂, H₂, NaOH

💡 Exam Tips

  • Hydroxide solubility ↑ down Group 2; sulfate solubility ↓ down Group 2
  • Halogens become LESS reactive down the group (F most reactive, I least)
  • Always add dilute HNO₃ before AgNO₃ in halide tests
  • Explain thermal decomposition using charge density and polarisation of anion
