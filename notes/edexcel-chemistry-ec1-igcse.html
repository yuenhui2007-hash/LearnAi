1. Overview

Atomic Structure & Periodicity covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IGCSE examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Atomic structure: protons, neutrons, electrons, mass and atomic numbers
  • Isotopes and relative atomic mass calculations
  • Electron configuration: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ notation
  • Periodic trends: atomic radius, ionisation energy, electronegativity
  • Blocks in periodic table: s, p, d, f blocks
  • Successive ionisation energies and evidence for shells
  • Periodic properties and explanations
