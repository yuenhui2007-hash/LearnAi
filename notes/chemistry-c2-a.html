Intermolecular Forces

ForceStrengthBetween
Van der WaalsWeakAll molecules
Dipole-dipoleModeratePolar molecules
Hydrogen bondingStrongH with N, O, or F
Hydrogen bonding explains why water has a high boiling point and ice is less dense than water.

📝 Quick Summary

  • Ionic: electron transfer between metal and non-metal
  • Covalent: electron sharing between non-metals
  • Metallic: delocalised electrons in metal lattice
  • Hydrogen bonding: H bonded to N, O, or F
  • Intermolecular forces affect physical properties

💡 Exam Tips

  • Always show electron transfer/sharing clearly
  • Mention the strong electrostatic attraction in ionic bonds
  • Hydrogen bonds are stronger than van der Waals but weaker than covalent bonds
  • Shape of molecule affects polarity
