0509 / 9715 — Speaking Tests and Presentation Skills

IGCSE Content

Speaking Test Format (0509)

  • Role-play (对话): Two brief scenarios testing transactional language (booking, shopping, asking for directions)
  • Topic conversation (话题讨论): Discussion of one prepared topic with follow-up questions
  • General conversation: Questions about self, family, school, hobbies, daily life

Preparation Strategies

  • Prepare 5–6 topics thoroughly: family, school, hobbies, hometown, future plans, favourite festival
  • For each topic, prepare: key vocabulary, opinions, reasons, personal examples
  • Practise with a partner or record yourself
  • Learn polite formulas: 请, 谢谢, 对不起, 我觉得..., 请问...

AS Level Content

Advanced Oral Skills

  • Opinion expression: 我认为..., 依我看来..., 不可否认的是..., 值得注意的是...
  • Hedging: 某种程度上, 一般来说, 或许, 可能 — softens claims for sophistication
  • Elaboration: Don't give one-word answers. Expand with 因为..., 例如..., 换句话说...
  • Discourse markers: 首先, 其次, 再者, 最后, 总而言之

Discussion and Debate Language

表达同意: 我同意...的观点, 确实如此, 我深有同感, 这一点毋庸置疑
表达不同意: 我不太同意..., 虽然...但是..., 从另一个角度来看..., 这可能有些片面
提出观点: 我认为..., 依我看..., 值得注意的是..., 有一个问题值得我们思考
举例说明: 例如, 比如, 以...为例, 众所周知
总结: 总而言之, 综上所述, 一言以蔽之

Presentation Skills

  • Structure: 开场白 → 主要内容(分点) → 总结 → 问答
  • 开场白: 大家好，今天我想谈谈... / 感谢各位，我演讲的题目是...
  • 过渡: 接下来..., 除此之外..., 另一方面..., 更重要的是...
  • 结束: 总之..., 谢谢大家, 欢迎提问

A2 Level Content

Analytical Discussion

  • At A2, oral exams may require discussion of literary or social topics
  • Analyse texts aloud: identify themes, techniques, and effects
  • Use literary terminology: 象征, 比喻, 对比, 主题, 人物塑造
  • Connect literature to broader social and historical contexts

Fluency Builders

  • Think-aloud fillers: 让我想想..., 这个问题很有意思..., 怎么说呢...
  • Self-correction: 不好意思，我想说的是... — shows awareness and control
  • Paraphrase: If you don't know a word, describe it another way
  • Speed control: Don't rush — pause between ideas for clarity

Last-Minute Summary

  • Prepare 5–6 core topics with vocabulary, opinions, and examples
  • Use polite formulas and discourse markers throughout
  • Expand answers — never give one-word responses
  • Express opinions and hedge for sophistication
  • Presentations: 开场白 → 分点内容 → 总结 → 问答
  • Use think-aloud fillers to buy thinking time
  • Self-correct smoothly if you make a mistake
  • Practise recording yourself and review for fluency and accuracy
  • For literary discussion: use 术语 and connect to context
  • Stay calm, speak clearly, make eye contact
