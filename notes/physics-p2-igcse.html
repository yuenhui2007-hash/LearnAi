📝 Quick Summary

  • SUVAT equations for constant acceleration
  • v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as
  • v-t graph: gradient = acceleration, area = displacement
  • s-t graph: gradient = velocity
  • Take care with signs (direction)

💡 Exam Tips

  • Choose the SUVAT equation with the missing variable
  • Draw graphs to visualise
  • Check units: all SI (m, s, m/s, m/s²)
  • Deceleration = negative acceleration
