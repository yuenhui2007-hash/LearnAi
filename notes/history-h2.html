IGCSE AS A2

The Holocaust

history-h2

📘 IGCSE Content

Definition

The systematic, state-sponsored persecution and murder of approximately 6 million Jews by Nazi Germany and collaborators between 1941 and 1945.

Stages of Persecution

  • 1933–35: Boycotts, Nuremberg Laws (stripped citizenship, banned intermarriage)
  • 1938: Kristallnacht — state-organised pogrom; 30,000 sent to concentration camps
  • 1939–41: Ghettoisation — Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow; forced labour
  • 1941–45: Final Solution — Einsatzgruppen (mass shootings), extermination camps (Auschwitz, Treblinka, Sobibor)

Key Events

  • Wannsee Conference (20 Jan 1942): Coordinated implementation of Final Solution
  • Auschwitz-Birkenau: Largest death camp; 1.1 million killed, mostly Jews

📗 AS Level Content

Ideological Roots

  • Anti-Semitism: Historical prejudice + pseudo-scientific racial theories
  • Volksgemeinschaft: Racial community excluding Jews
  • Lebensraum: Need for living space in East — Jews as obstacle

Role of Collaboration

  • Vichy France, Hungary, Romania — local administrations assisted deportations
  • Some resistance: Denmark saved most Jews; Bulgaria refused deportations

📙 A2 Level Content

Historiography

  • Intentionalists: Hitler planned genocide from early (Mein Kampf 1925)
  • Functionalists: Holocaust emerged from wartime radicalisation; cumulative radicalisation
  • Browning (Ordinary Men): Ordinary Germans participated — not just fanatics

Comparison & Significance

  • Unique or part of broader genocide? Comparison with other 20th century atrocities
  • Memory and memorialisation: Yad Vashem, Holocaust Memorial Day

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • Definition: Systematic murder of 6 million Jews (1941–45)
  • Stages: Persecution (1933) → Ghettos (1939) → Final Solution (1941)
  • Key event: Wannsee Conference (Jan 1942)
  • Intentionalist: Hitler planned it from start
  • Functionalist: Emerged from wartime conditions
  • Exam tip: Use specific camp names and dates; balance perpetrator and victim perspectives
