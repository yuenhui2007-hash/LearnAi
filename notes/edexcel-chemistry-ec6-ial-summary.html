Detailed Notes

1. Overview

Inorganic & Transition Metals covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel IAL examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Group 2 trends: reactivity, solubility of hydroxides and sulfates
  • Group 17 trends: oxidising power, displacement reactions
  • Transition metals: variable oxidation states, coloured compounds
  • Complex ions: ligands, coordination number, shapes
  • Catalytic properties of transition metals and compounds
  • Reactions of metal aqua ions with NaOH and NH₃
  • Qualitative analysis: flame tests and precipitation reactions

3. Important Formulas and Relationships

Key relationships and equations for Inorganic & Transition Metals are essential for problem-solving. Practice applying these to different contexts.

4. Common Exam Questions

Examiners frequently test:

  • Definitions and explanations of key terms
  • Application of concepts to novel situations
  • Analysis and evaluation of theories and models
  • Comparison between different approaches or periods
