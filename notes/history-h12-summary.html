International Relations, 1871–1945

Cambridge 9389 A-Level / 0470 IGCSE — Last-Minute Revision

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1871–1914: Bismarck's alliance system → Wilhelm II's Weltpolitik → Triple Alliance vs Triple Entente → Sarajevo (28 June 1914).
  • WWI: Western Front stalemate; USA enters April 1917; armistice 11 Nov 1918.
  • 1919: Versailles — Article 231 (war guilt), reparations, demilitarisation, League of Nations. USA never joins.
  • 1920s: League successes (Aaland, Upper Silesia); Locarno Era (1925); Kellogg-Briand (1928). Failures: Corfu (1923).
  • 1931: Manchuria — League ineffective. 1935: Abyssinia — sanctions fail; Hoare-Laval Pact.
  • 1933–39: Hitler leaves League, rearm, Rhineland (1936), Anschluss (1938), Munich (Sept 1938), Czech occupation (March 1939), Nazi-Soviet Pact (Aug 1939), Poland invaded (1 Sept 1939).
  • WWII: Blitzkrieg 1939–41; Barbarossa (June 1941); Pearl Harbor (Dec 1941); turning points 1942–43; D-Day (June 1944); atomic bombs (Aug 1945).
  • Historiography: Versailles (Keynes harsh vs Taylor not too harsh); League (structurally doomed vs powers failed it); appeasement (Churchill vs revisionist/realist).
  • Exam Tip: Chronology of 1930s crises is critical: 1931→1935→1936→1938→1939. Distinguish WWI causes (systemic/mis calculation) from WWII (aggressive dictatorships).
