International Relations, 1871–1945

history-h12

📘 IGCSE Content

Bismarck's System (1871–90)

  • Three Emperors' League (1873): Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary
  • Dual Alliance (1879): Germany-Austria — against Russia
  • Triple Alliance (1882): Added Italy
  • Reinsurance Treaty (1887): Secret Germany-Russia agreement

New Imperialism

  • Scramble for Africa (1884–85): Berlin Conference; Congo Free State
  • Boer War (1899–1902): Britain vs South African republics; concentration camps
  • Open Door Policy (1899): US access to China markets

World War I

  • Causes: Militarism, alliances, imperialism, nationalism
  • 1914–18: Trench warfare; Western Front stalemate
  • US entry (1917): Unrestricted submarine warfare; Zimmerman Telegram

Interwar Period

  • League of Nations: US never joined; failed to prevent aggression
  • Appeasement: Munich (1938); failed to stop Hitler
  • 1939: WWII begins

📗 AS Level Content

Peace Settlements

  • Treaty of Versailles (1919): War guilt (Article 231), reparations, territorial losses, disarmament
  • Other treaties: St Germain (Austria), Trianon (Hungary), Neuilly (Bulgaria), Sèvres (Turkey)
  • Criticisms: Too harsh (Keynes) vs justified (Clemenceau)

📙 A2 Level Content

Why Did Peace Fail?

  • Structural flaws: League without US; no enforcement mechanism
  • Economic instability: Reparations, hyperinflation, Great Depression
  • Rise of totalitarianism: Hitler, Mussolini — exploited Versailles grievances

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1871–90: Bismarck's alliance system
  • 1884–85: Scramble for Africa
  • 1914–18: WWI
  • 1919: Treaty of Versailles
  • 1920s: League of Nations; Locarno era
  • 1930s: Depression; rise of dictators; appeasement
  • 1939: WWII begins
