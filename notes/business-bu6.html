Finance & Accounting

9609 · 0450

Detailed Notes

1. Sources of Finance

  • Internal: Retained profit, sale of assets.
  • External: Share capital, loans, overdrafts, trade credit, leasing, venture capital.
  • Short-term: Overdraft, trade credit, factoring.
  • Long-term: Shares, loans, debentures, mortgages.

2. Cash Flow

Cash inflows - cash outflows = net cash flow. Opening + net = closing.

3. Break-Even

Break-even = Fixed costs / (Price - Variable cost per unit)

4. Financial Statements

  • Income statement: Revenue - COGS = Gross profit - Expenses = Net profit.
  • Statement of financial position: Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
