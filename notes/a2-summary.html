0452 / 9706 — The Debit-Credit System

Last-Minute Summary

  • DEAD CLIC: Debits increase Expenses, Assets, Drawings; Credits increase Liabilities, Income, Capital
  • Every transaction = at least one debit and one credit of equal value
  • Assets: Debit ↑ Credit ↓ | Liabilities: Debit ↓ Credit ↑ | Capital: Debit ↓ Credit ↑
  • Income: Credit ↑ | Expenses: Debit ↑
  • Balance c/d on the smaller side; balance b/d on the opposite side
  • Trade discount: not recorded; Cash discount: Discount Allowed (expense) / Discount Received (income)
  • Accruals: owe but not paid; Prepayments: paid but not yet used
  • Irrecoverable debt: remove from receivables; Allowance: estimate future bad debts
  • Inventory at lower of cost and NRV
