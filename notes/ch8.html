Cultural Context (A2)

9715 A2 — History, Society, and Culture in Chinese-Speaking Regions

IGCSE Content

Cultural context as a dedicated A2 topic builds on general knowledge developed at IGCSE through reading Chinese texts and learning about Chinese-speaking regions.

AS Level Content

AS Level introduces some cultural and historical context through literary texts, but comprehensive cultural study is an A2 requirement.

A2 Level Content

Key Historical Periods

  • 晚清 (Late Qing, 1840–1911): Opium Wars, Taiping Rebellion, Self-Strengthening Movement, Boxer Rebellion, fall of imperial system
  • 民国时期 (Republican Era, 1912–1949): Warlord period, May Fourth Movement (1919), Nanjing Decade, Sino-Japanese War, Civil War
  • 建国初期 (Early PRC, 1949–1966): Land reform, Korean War, Great Leap Forward, collectivisation
  • 文革时期 (Cultural Revolution, 1966–1976): Red Guards, persecution, disruption of education and culture
  • 改革开放 (Reform and Opening, 1978–present): Economic transformation, Tiananmen 1989, rise as global power

Social Issues

  • 人口与计划生育: One-child policy (1979–2015), ageing population, gender imbalance
  • 城乡差距: Urban-rural divide, 户口 (hukou) system, migrant workers (农民工)
  • 教育: Exam pressure (高考), inequality between regions, study abroad trends
  • 环境: Pollution, renewable energy, climate policy, ecological civilisation (生态文明)
  • 科技: Internet censorship, AI development, digital payment society, tech giants

Cultural Concepts

儒家思想 (Confucianism):
- 仁 (benevolence), 义 (righteousness), 礼 (ritual/propriety)
- 孝道 (filial piety), 等级秩序 (hierarchical order)
- Influence on education, family structure, social harmony

道家思想 (Daoism):
- 道 (the Way), 无为 (non-action), 自然 (naturalness)
- Balance, harmony with nature, simplicity

面子 (Face / Miànzi):
- Social standing, reputation, dignity
- Central to social interactions and conflict avoidance

关系 (Guānxì):
- Personal connections and networks
- Importance in business, politics, and daily life

集体主义 vs 个人主义:
- Group harmony and duty over individual desires
- Contrast with Western individualism

Regional Diversity

  • 中国大陆: Mandarin dominance, regional cuisines, ethnic minorities
  • 台湾: Democracy, indigenous cultures, cross-strait relations, Hokkien/Hakka heritage
  • 香港: Cantonese culture, colonial legacy, "one country two systems", 1997 handover
  • 新加坡: Multilingual society, bilingual policy, multiculturalism
  • 海外华人: Diaspora communities, cultural preservation, identity

Applying Cultural Context to Texts

  • How does historical background shape the author's perspective?
  • What cultural values are embodied or challenged in the text?
  • How might readers from different cultural backgrounds interpret the text differently?
  • What social issues does the text reflect or critique?

Last-Minute Summary

  • Know key periods: 晚清, 民国, 建国初期, 文革, 改革开放
  • Social issues: 人口, 城乡差距, 教育, 环境, 科技
  • Cultural concepts: 儒家, 道家, 面子, 关系, 集体主义
  • Regional diversity: 大陆, 台湾, 香港, 新加坡, 海外华人
  • Apply context to texts: author perspective, cultural values, reader interpretation, social critique
  • Use cultural knowledge to deepen literary analysis — not just name-drop facts
  • Be sensitive and balanced when discussing politically sensitive topics
  • Connect historical events to their representation in literature
