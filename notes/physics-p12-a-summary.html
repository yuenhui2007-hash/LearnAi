Quantum & Particle Physics

9702 (A-Level) · 0625 (IGCSE)

Last-Minute Revision Summary

  • Quarks: u(+2/3 e), d(-1/3 e), s(-1/3 e), c(+2/3 e), t(+2/3 e), b(-1/3 e); antiquarks have opposite signs
  • Hadrons = made of quarks (baryons = 3q, mesons = q+anti-q); Leptons = fundamental (e, mu, tau, neutrinos)
  • Conserved in ALL interactions: charge Q, baryon number B, lepton number L
  • Strangeness conserved in strong/EM, can change by +/- 1 in weak interactions
  • Proton = uud; Neutron = udd; pi+ = u anti-d; pi- = d anti-u; K+ = u anti-s
  • Exchange particles: EM = photon, Strong = gluon, Weak = W+/W-/Z0, Gravity = graviton (theoretical)
  • Beta- decay: d to u + e- + anti-nu_e (mediated by W-); Beta+ decay: u to d + e+ + nu_e (mediated by W+)

Key Formulas

Q_initial = Q_final
B_initial = B_final
L_initial = L_final

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing baryons (3 quarks) with mesons (quark + antiquark)
  • Forgetting that leptons are fundamental -- they have no quark structure
  • Not checking strangeness conservation in strong interactions
  • Giving mesons baryon number of +1 instead of 0

Quick Exam Tips

  • Always check all three conservation laws (Q, B, L) for particle interactions
  • Quark charges: up-type (u,c,t) = +2/3 e; down-type (d,s,b) = -1/3 e
  • Baryons have B = +1; mesons have B = 0; antiparticles have opposite quantum numbers
  • Strange particles produced in pairs (strong) but decay singly (weak)
  • When writing quark equations for beta decay, include the exchange boson (W- or W+) for full marks
