Exam Tips

  • Read the question carefully — identify exactly what is being asked.
  • Show all working — method marks are often awarded even if the final answer is wrong.
  • Check units — ensure your final answer has the correct unit.
  • Use the mark scheme structure — answer in the format the examiner expects.
  • Manage your time — don't spend too long on any single question.
