Organic Synthesis & Analysis

9701 (A-Level) · 0620 (IGCSE)

Last-Minute Revision Summary

  • Retrosynthesis: work backward
  • LiAlH₄ stronger than NaBH₄
  • Recrystallisation for purification

Quick Exam Tips

  • PCC oxidises 1° alcohol to aldehyde (stops there)
  • LiAlH₄ is stronger than NaBH₄ — can reduce esters and acids
  • In retrosynthesis, identify bond disconnections strategically
  • Always consider regioselectivity and stereochemistry in synthesis

📚 Additional Revision Points

  • Transition metals: partially filled d orbitals in ions; variable oxidation states; coloured; catalytic
  • 4s fills before 3d, but 4s empties before 3d in ions; exceptions: Cr (3d⁵4s¹), Cu (3d¹⁰4s¹)
  • Complex ions: metal centre + ligands; shapes: linear (2), tetrahedral/square planar (4), octahedral (6)
  • Colour from d-d transitions; ligand field splitting depends on ligand (spectrochemical series)
  • Stronger field ligand → larger splitting → absorbs higher energy → different colour
  • Cr₂O₇²⁻ (orange) → Cr³⁺ (green); MnO₄⁻ (purple) → Mn²⁺ (colourless)
  • Heterogeneous catalysts: Fe, V₂O₅, Ni; work by adsorption mechanism
  • Ligand substitution, precipitation, redox are key reaction types

💡 More Exam Tips

  • Write 4s before 3d for atom; remove 4s first for ions
  • Zn is NOT a transition element (full d subshell in Zn²⁺)
  • Colour explanations: mention d-orbital splitting, electron promotion, complementary colour
  • Balance redox equations with H⁺/OH⁻ and H₂O; check atoms and charges
