IGCSE AS A2

Britain, 1918–1951

history-h10

📘 IGCSE Content

Post-WWI Britain

  • Armistice (Nov 1918): "Land fit for heroes" — promises unfulfilled
  • Economic problems: Unemployment, debt, declining industries (coal, textiles, shipbuilding)
  • General Strike (1926): Miners' dispute escalated to national strike; 9 days; TUC called it off

Interwar Politics

  • Lloyd George Coalition (1918–22): Post-war reconstruction; Irish independence
  • Labour Government (1924, 1929–31): First Labour administration; MacDonald
  • National Government (1931–40): Coalition led by MacDonald; austerity measures

World War II

  • Appeasement: Chamberlain; Munich Agreement (1938)
  • 1939: Britain declares war on Germany
  • Churchill becomes PM (May 1940): "Blood, toil, tears and sweat"
  • Battle of Britain (1940): RAF victory; prevented German invasion
  • Atlantic Charter (1941): Anglo-American war aims

📗 AS Level Content

Social Change

  • Women's suffrage (1918, 1928): Vote extended to all women over 21
  • Housing: Suburban expansion; "Homes fit for heroes"
  • Broadcasting: BBC founded (1922); radio transformed culture

Economic Depression

  • Wall Street Crash impact: Unemployment reached 3 million (1932)
  • Jarrow March (1936): Unemployed shipyard workers marched to London
  • Recovery: Rearmament boosted economy from 1937

📙 A2 Level Content

Post-War Transformation

  • Labour landslide (1945): Attlee government; welfare state
  • Beveridge Report (1942): Blueprint for welfare state; "Five Giants" — want, disease, ignorance, squalor, idleness
  • NHS (1948): Free healthcare at point of use
  • Nationalisation: Coal, rail, steel, electricity, gas

Decline of Empire

  • Indian independence (1947): Partition into India and Pakistan
  • End of British Raj: Mountbatten as last Viceroy
  • Palestine: British withdrawal (1948); UN partition plan

📝 Last-Minute Summary

  • 1918: WWI ends; Lloyd George promises "land fit for heroes"
  • 1926: General Strike
  • 1928: Equal suffrage for women
  • 1931–40: National Government; Depression; appeasement
  • 1940: Churchill PM; Battle of Britain
  • 1945: Labour landslide; Attlee government
  • 1948: NHS established
