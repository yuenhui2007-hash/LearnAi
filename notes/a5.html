Trial Balance

0452 / 9706 — Checking the Arithmetical Accuracy of Ledgers

IGCSE Content

Purpose of a Trial Balance

A trial balance is a list of all ledger account balances at a specific date. It checks the arithmetical accuracy of the double-entry bookkeeping. If total debits equal total credits, the ledger is mathematically correct (though errors like omission or wrong account may still exist).

How to Extract a Trial Balance

  1. Balance every ledger account
  2. Determine whether each balance is a debit or credit:
    • Assets, Expenses, Drawings → Debit balances
    • Liabilities, Income, Capital → Credit balances
  3. List all balances in debit and credit columns
  4. Total both columns — they MUST agree

AS Level Content

Limitations of the Trial Balance

A balanced trial balance does NOT guarantee error-free accounts. Errors NOT revealed:

  • Error of omission: Entire transaction not recorded
  • Error of commission: Correct amount, wrong account (same category)
  • Error of principle: Correct amount, wrong category (e.g., asset vs expense)
  • Compensating errors: Two errors that cancel each other
  • Original entry error: Same wrong figure in both debit and credit
  • Reversal: Debits and credits swapped but equal

Suspense Account

When the trial balance doesn't balance:

  1. Open a Suspense Account with the difference
  2. If debit total > credit total: Suspense Account has credit balance
  3. If credit total > debit total: Suspense Account has debit balance
  4. Find and correct errors, clearing the suspense account
Example: Debit column = $50,200; Credit column = $49,800
Difference = $400 (debits higher)
→ Suspense Account credited with $400
When error found (e.g., cash sale of $400 not recorded):
Dr Cash $400, Cr Suspense $400
Suspense account now = $0

A2 Level Content

Extended Trial Balance (ETB)

An extended trial balance is used to prepare financial statements and includes adjustment columns:

  • Start with unadjusted trial balance
  • Add adjustments in separate debit/credit columns (accruals, prepayments, depreciation, bad debts, etc.)
  • Calculate extended balances
  • Sort into Statement of Profit or Loss and Statement of Financial Position columns
Extended Trial Balance Columns:
1. Unadjusted TB (Dr/Cr)
2. Adjustments (Dr/Cr)
3. Extended TB (Dr/Cr)
4. Income Statement (Dr/Cr)
5. Statement of Financial Position (Dr/Cr)
