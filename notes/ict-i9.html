IGCSE AS A2

Impact of ICT on Society

ict-i9

📘 IGCSE Content

Positive Impacts

  • Communication: Email, social media, video calls
  • Education: Online learning, digital resources
  • Healthcare: Telemedicine, electronic records
  • Commerce: E-commerce, global markets
  • Entertainment: Streaming, gaming, social media

Negative Impacts

  • Job displacement: Automation replacing workers
  • Privacy concerns: Data collection, surveillance
  • Digital divide: Gap between those with/without access
  • Health issues: Screen time, cyberbullying, addiction

📗 AS Level Content

Ethical Issues

  • Privacy vs security: Government surveillance debate
  • Intellectual property: Copyright, piracy, plagiarism
  • AI bias: Algorithms reflecting societal prejudices

Environmental Impact

  • E-waste: Discarded electronics
  • Energy consumption: Data centres use significant power
  • Positive: Remote work reduces travel

📙 A2 Level Content

Future of Work

  • Gig economy: Freelance platforms
  • Remote work: Enabled by ICT
  • AI automation: New jobs created, others lost
  • Skills gap: Need for digital literacy
