Bank Reconciliation — Last-Minute Revision

0452 / 9706 — Reconciling the Cash Book with the Bank Statement

Last-Minute Summary

  • Purpose: Explain difference between cash book and bank statement balances
  • Always update cash book first for bank charges, interest, direct debits, direct credits, dishonoured cheques
  • Unpresented cheques: deduct from bank statement (business has paid, bank hasn't)
  • Uncredited deposits: add to bank statement (business has received, bank hasn't cleared)
  • Bank errors: adjust reconciliation but NOT cash book
  • Cash book errors: correct in cash book before reconciliation
  • Overdraft: credit balance in cash book = debit on bank statement; current liability
  • Final reconciled balance MUST match updated cash book balance
  • Bank charges/interest → expense; dishonoured cheques → reverse receivable receipt
