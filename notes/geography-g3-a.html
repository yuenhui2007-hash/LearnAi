Congruence & Similarity

geography-g3

📘 IGCSE Content

Congruence

Two shapes are congruent if they have exactly the same shape and size.

  • SSS: Three sides equal
  • SAS: Two sides and included angle equal
  • ASA: Two angles and included side equal
  • RHS: Right angle, hypotenuse, one side equal (right triangles only)

Similarity

Two shapes are similar if they have the same shape but different sizes.

  • AA: Two angles equal
  • SAS (similarity): Two sides in proportion and included angle equal
  • SSS (similarity): All sides in proportion

Properties of Similar Triangles

  • Corresponding angles are equal
  • Corresponding sides are in proportion
  • Ratio of areas = (scale factor)²
  • Ratio of volumes = (scale factor)³

📗 AS Level Content

Proofs of Congruence Conditions

  • SSS proof: Unique triangle determined by three sides
  • SAS proof: Two sides and included angle fix the triangle

Applications

  • Shadow problems: Using similar triangles to find heights
  • Map scales: 1:n ratio

📙 A2 Level Content

Advanced Similarity

  • Self-similarity: Fractals; Sierpinski triangle
  • Spiral similarity: Combination of rotation and dilation

📐 Worked Example

Question: Find the interior angle of a regular decagon.

Solution:
n = 10
Interior angle = (n − 2) × 180° / n
= (10 − 2) × 180° / 10 = 8 × 18° = 144°
Answer: 144°
Confusing interior and exterior angles. Exterior angles of ANY polygon always sum to 360°.
For regular polygons: interior + exterior = 180°. Use this to check your answers.

⚡ Quick Recall

  • Interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°
  • Exterior angle sum = 360°
  • Regular polygon interior = (n−2)×180°/n
