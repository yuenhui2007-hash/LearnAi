1. Overview

Boolean Algebra & Logic covers fundamental concepts essential for Edexcel Computer Science examinations. Understanding these principles thoroughly will provide a strong foundation for answering both structured and extended-response questions.

2. Key Concepts

  • Logic gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR
  • Truth tables for all gates
  • Boolean algebra laws: commutative, associative, distributive
  • De Morgan's theorems
  • Simplifying Boolean expressions
  • Karnaugh maps for simplification
  • Applications: adders, multiplexers, flip-flops
