Euclidean Geometry Fundamentals

geography-g1

📘 IGCSE Content

Basic Definitions

  • Point: A location in space with no size
  • Line: Infinite set of points extending in both directions
  • Plane: Flat, two-dimensional surface extending infinitely
  • Line segment: Part of a line between two points
  • Ray: Line extending from a point in one direction infinitely

Axioms & Postulates

  • Euclid's 5th Postulate (Parallel Postulate): Given a line and a point not on it, exactly one parallel line can be drawn through the point
  • Non-Euclidean geometries: Spherical (no parallels), Hyperbolic (many parallels)

Notation

  • AB̄ denotes line segment from A to B
  • ∠ABC denotes angle at vertex B
  • △ABC denotes triangle with vertices A, B, C

📗 AS Level Content

Proof Techniques

  • Direct proof: Assume premises, derive conclusion logically
  • Proof by contradiction: Assume opposite, show contradiction
  • Proof by contrapositive: Prove ¬Q → ¬P instead of P → Q

Logical Structure

  • Theorem: Statement proved true
  • Corollary: Result that follows directly from a theorem
  • Lemma: Minor result used to prove a larger theorem

📙 A2 Level Content

Advanced Foundations

  • Vector space approach: Points as vectors; lines as affine subspaces
  • Coordinate-free geometry: Using inner products and distances

📐 Worked Example

Question: Find the interior angle of a regular decagon.

Solution:
n = 10
Interior angle = (n − 2) × 180° / n
= (10 − 2) × 180° / 10 = 8 × 18° = 144°
Answer: 144°
Confusing interior and exterior angles. Exterior angles of ANY polygon always sum to 360°.
For regular polygons: interior + exterior = 180°. Use this to check your answers.

⚡ Quick Recall

  • Interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°
  • Exterior angle sum = 360°
  • Regular polygon interior = (n−2)×180°/n
