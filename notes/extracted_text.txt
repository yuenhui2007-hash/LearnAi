IAS 10: Events after the reporting period 
These are events, either favourable or unfavourable, thatoccur between the end of the reporting periodand the date on which the financial statements are authorised for issue. Theymay occur as a result of information which becomes available after the end of the periodand therefore need to be disclosed in thefinancial statementsThe key is the point intime at which changes to the financial statements can be made.
