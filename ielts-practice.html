Quick Tips for Test Day

🎧 Listening

Read questions before the audio starts. Listen for signpost words like "firstly", "however", "in conclusion". Check your spelling — incorrect spelling loses marks.

📖 Reading

Skim passages first for main ideas. Don't get stuck on one question — move on and return later. The answers appear in order in the text.

✍️ Writing

Plan before you write. Task 2 carries double the marks of Task 1. Leave 5 minutes to proofread. Aim for variety in vocabulary and sentence structure.

🎤 Speaking

Don't memorise scripts — examiners can tell. Use the 1-minute prep time for bullet points. Extend answers with reasons and examples. It's okay to ask for clarification.
