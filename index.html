The Problem

Traditional Education Has Critical Gaps

📚

One-Size-Fits-All

Every student receives the same textbooks, homework, and exercises. But every student learns differently.

💰

Expensive Tutoring

Private tutors are costly and unavailable 24/7. Students need instant explanations and personalized practice.

⏱️

Time Wasted

Students spend hours making notes, flashcards, and searching for practice questions instead of actually learning.

How It Works

From Upload to Mastery in 5 Steps

01
📤

Upload Learning Materials

Upload textbooks, lecture notes, past papers, and syllabus documents. The AI analyzes key concepts, topics, exam requirements, and question patterns.

02
📝

AI Creates Study Resources

Automatically generates personalized notes, flashcards, mind maps, and revision sheets tailored to your format preference.

03
✅

AI Homework & Exam Marking

Upload essays, calculations, and past papers. The AI marks answers, identifies mistakes, explains errors, and provides model answers.

04
📅

Personal AI Study Planner

Creates a personalized revision schedule based on exam dates, current grades, weak topics, and available study hours. Auto-adjusts as you improve.

05
🎨

Custom Learning Materials

The AI creates entirely new educational materials — personalized workbooks, worksheets, and practice exams based on your specific needs and interests.

Features

Powered by Advanced AI

🧬

AI Digital Twin

The AI builds a "digital twin" of each student — learning strengths, weaknesses, learning style, and memory retention over time.

🗺️

Adaptive Learning Path

Creates personalized sequences based on prerequisite knowledge. Two students studying the same syllabus get completely different paths.

🔮

AI Exam Predictor

Predicts likely exam topics based on syllabus coverage, historical papers, and your current readiness.

🌡️

Weakness Heatmap

Visual map of every syllabus objective showing confidence, mastery, and forgetting risk at a glance.

🧠

Memory Decay Engine

Uses spaced repetition and forgetting curves to schedule revision before knowledge fades.

🏆

AI Past Paper Coach

Analyzes why marks were lost and identifies recurring patterns — poor evaluation, missing keywords, calculation errors.

🤝

AI Study Companion

Acts like a coach that checks in daily, adjusts schedules, provides motivation, and keeps students accountable.

📖

Explain at Any Level

Instantly explain any topic at primary school, high school, university, or expert level.

📚

Mistake Library

Every mistake is stored and categorized. The AI regularly creates quizzes based only on past errors.
