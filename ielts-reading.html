IELTS Reading

3 passages ° 40 questions ° 60 minutes

Test Format

  • Academic: Three long texts from books, journals, magazines, and newspapers
  • General Training: Sections 1 & 2: everyday topics; Section 3: general interest topic
  • Texts range from descriptive and factual to discursive and analytical

Question Types

  • Multiple choice
  • Identifying information (True/False/Not Given)
  • Identifying writer's views/claims (Yes/No/Not Given)
  • Matching information, headings, features, sentence endings
  • Sentence completion, summary completion, note completion
  • Table/flow-chart completion, diagram label completion
  • Short-answer questions

⏆� Top Tips

  • Skim the passage first to get the main idea
  • Scan for specific information when answering questions
  • Pay attention to synonyms – the answer may use different words
  • Manage your time – 20 minutes per passage
  • Transfer answers carefully to the answer sheet

Scoring

Each correct answer receives 1 mark. Scores are converted to the IELTS 9-band scale.

Academic Example: 30 correct = Band 7.0
