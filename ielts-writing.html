IELTS Writing

2 tasks ° 60 minutes

Task 1 (20 minutes ´ 150 words minimum)

Academic: Describe, summarise or explain information from a graph, table, chart, or diagram.

General Training: Write a letter requesting information or explaining a situation (formal, semi-formal, or informal).

Task 2 (40 minutes ´ 250 words minimum)

Write an essay in response to a point of view, argument, or problem.

This task contributes twice as much to the final Writing score as Task 1.

Assessment Criteria

  • Task Achievement/Response: Did you answer the question fully?
  • Coherence and Cohesion: Is your writing well-organised with clear paragraphs?
  • Lexical Resource: Did you use a wide range of vocabulary accurately?
  • Grammatical Range and Accuracy: Did you use varied sentence structures correctly?

⏇�� Top Tips

  • Plan before you write; spend 5 minutes on Task 2 outline
  • Use paragraphing clearly: introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion
  • Avoid repetition 〪 use synonyms and varied vocabulary
  • Don't leave 5 minutes at the end to proofread
